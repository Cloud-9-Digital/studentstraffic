Students Traffic helps you choose where to study abroad with clear information and real people to talk to.

EXPLORE UNIVERSITIES, COUNTRIES AND PROGRAMS
Browse study destinations, search programs and open detailed university pages. See programmes, tuition fees, teaching language, intake months, admissions, hostel and accommodation, safety and student support in one place.

ASK MIRA, YOUR STUDY-ABROAD ASSISTANT
Ask Mira about fees, programmes, hostel life or how universities compare. Get quick answers grounded in the university profiles in the app, then confirm the details with a counsellor.

TALK TO STUDENT GUIDES
Message students who already study abroad and ask about campus life, food, costs and what to expect before you apply.

GET FREE COUNSELLING
Share your details and our counsellors will call you with guidance on country options, scholarships, shortlisting and your next admission step.

PLAN YOUR APPLICATIONS
Save universities, compare options and keep track of your applications from one place.

STAY UPDATED
Turn on notifications to hear back from student guides and counsellors.

Fees, intakes and requirements can change, so always confirm the latest details with the university before you apply.
