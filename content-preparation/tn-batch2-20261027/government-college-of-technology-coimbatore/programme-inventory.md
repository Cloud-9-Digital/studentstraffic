## Fee interpretation

The dated 2026–27 NFG/BC/MBC certificate gives tuition INR 2,000 and college charges totalling INR 6,780. Its introductory “entire course” wording is ambiguous; the independently reviewed current-linked fee-table page 1 explicitly labels tuition INR 2,000 as **per annum**. The payload therefore uses this narrow, two-source annual tuition determination, never multiplies a combined total or claims an all-inclusive price. The fee-table PDF has a 2025-10-31 creation date and its older first-year combined total is deliberately not reused as a 2026–27 quote.

The category certificate separately states hostel charges INR 7,000/year and approximate boarding INR 2,800/month; its own eleven-month estimate is INR 37,800. This is not four-year total cost. First-graduate/SC/ST concession schedule is separate, not an unconditional zero-fee offer.
