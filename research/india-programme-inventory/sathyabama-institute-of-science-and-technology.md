## Identity and merge scope

- Deemed to be university under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956; UGC consolidated list (as on 25.01.2023)
  entry 815, "Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, Jeppiaar Nagar, Rajiv Gandhi Salai,
  Chennai-600 119", date 16.07.2001. Source: https://www.ugc.gov.in/oldpdf/Consolidated%20list%20of%20All%20Universities.pdf
- Merged into one page: Chennai main campus; Sathyabama Dental College and Hospital (Chennai campus,
  School of Dental Sciences); School of Law (Chennai); Sriperumbudur (Vadamangalam, Kanchipuram district)
  off-campus. The 2026 prospectus (p. 9) says the Sriperumbudur campus opened from 2025-26 with UGC and
  Ministry of Education approval, on 78 acres. Jeppiaar University and other Jeppiaar institutions excluded.
- The founding trust dates to 1988 (prospectus, GRUP 2026 data); deemed status dates to 2001.

## Why the university is held

1. **Intake / class start (blocking every programme).** No official 2026-27 class-start date was found.
   `https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/student/academic-calendar` reads "Will be updated soon". The 2019/2023
   academic regulations only say odd-semester end-semester exams fall in October-December and even-semester
   exams in April-May (B.P.T.: February-March and August-September). The admission pages, prospectus,
   FAQ, international-admissions page and newsletters give no commencement date. The validator requires
   verified intake evidence and an `intakeCodes` month, so nothing can be packaged without guessing.
2. **Fees.** No public tuition fee for any programme. `https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/fees_` and the
   "Fee (2026-2027)" / "First Year Fee" links go to `feeportal.sathyabama.ac.in` (login-only SPA). The
   older `sist.sathyabama.ac.in/fees2/*.jpg` links on the admission pages return 404. The 2026
   prospectus has no fee table (40 pages checked). Only Ph.D. fees are public (FAQ). This alone would
   allow `on_request` fees, but combined with (1) the page would have no verified intake and no fee.
3. **Regulator and badge sources unreachable from this container (2026-10-23, two attempts each):**
   naac.gov.in (connection reset), nirfindia.org (HTTP 403), nbaind.org (HTTP 403), pci.gov.in and
   coa.gov.in and ncte.gov.in (TLS failure at the egress proxy), pci.nic.in (connection reset). No NAAC,
   NIRF or NBA badge could be verified; pharmacy, architecture and B.Ed. programmes would be held.

**To unblock:** an official 2026-27 (or 2027-28) academic calendar or admission notice with a
class-start date. Then re-run with fees `on_request` (Grade A evidence: the fee page linking only to
the login portal) unless a public fee table appears.

## Verified facts ready for a later pass

  - UG B.E./B.Tech. and UG B.A./B.Sc. academic regulations: "The medium of instruction, examinations and
    project report will be in English Language throughout the Programme".
    https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/sites/default/files/2023-04/ACADEMIC%20REGULATION%20UG%20B.E.B.TECH_.pdf,
    https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/sites/default/files/2023-04/ACADEMIC%20REGULATION%20UG%20B.A.B.SC_.pdf
  - Regulations 2023 (clause 5.6 "Medium of Instruction", English) for M.E. CSE, M.Tech. Medical
    Instrumentation, B.Des., M.B.A., B.B.A., M.C.A., M.Sc. Mathematics, M.A. English (except language
    courses); law (B.A. LL.B., LL.B., LL.M.): "The medium of instruction and examination shall be in
    English only"; B.P.T. clause 5.5 English. URL pattern:
    `https://sist.sathyabama.ac.in/syllabus2023/<school>/<course><branch>regulation.pdf`, e.g.
    `soc/mecse-r2023regulation.pdf`, `sol/llbll.b-r2023regulation.pdf`, `soms/mbamba-r2023regulation.pdf`.
  - B.Pharm. Regulations 2026 (syllabus.sathyabama.ac.in/syllabus2026/assets/regulation/5.pdf): English;
    odd semesters June/July-November/December.
  - Not found for B.Sc. Nursing, B.D.S., M.D.S., M.Pharm., Pharm.D. regulations.
  - Study in India partner status could not be confirmed (institute list is JS-rendered).
- **International route:** https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/International_Student_Admissions: students from
  other countries may apply for bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes by e-mailing the application
  form (https://www.sathyabama.ac.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/2-Application-Form-Study-in-Sathyabama_0.doc)

  p. 22, FAQ). BDS: NEET enrolment mandatory / NEET counselling (prospectus pp. 33, 37). Law: institution
  entrance exam (prospectus p. 36). MDS: Indian nationals, PIO or OCI only, NEET-MDS and central counselling.
- **Eligibility:** programme tables at /ug_eligibility, /Eligibility (engineering, B.Arch., B.Des.),
  /pg_eligibility and /campuses/{ug,pg}_eligibility, /campuses/Eligibility. These state 50% aggregate
