# Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham — programme inventory sources

## Sources used
- Official programme directory via Amrita's WordPress REST API (curl-accessible, not visible in the static HTML shell): https://www.amrita.edu/wp-json/wp/v2/program (custom `program` post type, 428 total entries paginated at `per_page=100`), cross-resolved against its three taxonomies — https://www.amrita.edu/wp-json/wp/v2/degree (Undergraduate/Postgraduate/Integrated Degree/Doctoral/Diploma/Fellowship/Certificate/International/Live-in-Labs/Dual Degree/etc.), https://www.amrita.edu/wp-json/wp/v2/campus-name (10 campuses: Amaravati, Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Chennai, Coimbatore, Faridabad, Haridwar, Kochi, Mysuru, Nagercoil) and https://www.amrita.edu/wp-json/wp/v2/interest (34 discipline tags). This is the same data source that drives the human-facing https://www.amrita.edu/academics/ listing (36 pages in the UI).
- https://www.amrita.edu/international-admission/ — explicit statement that Amrita admits international (PIO/OCI/NRI/NRI-sponsored) students to "all its 200+ undergraduate and post graduate programs," with a named exception: Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing and Ayurveda additionally require an Amrita entrance exam for international applicants.
- Existing bundle payload for cross-check: content-migrations/0093-india-amrita-vishwa-vidyapeetham/payload.json (4 canonical courses on site today: be-btech-computer-science-engineering, be-btech-electronics-communication-engineering, be-btech-electrical-electronics-engineering, mba — the last mapped to the MBA/MBA-MS Business Analytics dual degree with University at Buffalo).
- Canonical course slugs: grepped from `content-migrations/*/payload.json` (51 distinct slugs).
- NOT completed in this pass: Study in India portal, AICTE facilities dashboard, the NIRF institutional data PDF (Amrita submits per-campus NIRF data separately), and the Shiksha/Collegedunia/Careers360 checklist. Given the REST API already returned Amrita's full, current, first-party programme catalogue, the aggregator checklist was judged lower-value for this pass but is still recommended before publishing, specifically to sanity-check for programmes the REST API might list under an unexpected post status.

## Already on site vs missing
- Already on site: 3 of 4 existing canonical offerings matched cleanly to source rows (B.Tech CSE, B.Tech ECE, B.Tech EEE). The 4th, the MBA/MBA-MS Business Analytics dual degree with University at Buffalo, was not found as a distinct row in the `program` post type in this pass — likely listed elsewhere on the site (an international-partnerships or Amrita Online page) rather than in the main programme catalogue; needs a direct check before assuming it's missing from the source list rather than from our matching.
- Missing (open_to_international but not on site): 255 rows. Amrita's real scale here is the story: this is a 10-campus, 200+ live-programme university and the site currently carries 4 offerings, all Coimbatore-adjacent engineering plus one dual MBA. Entire disciplines are absent — all of Medicine (MBBS), Dentistry, Ayurveda, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law (LL.B./LL.M.), Architecture, Business (MBA variants, BBA, commerce), Computing (MCA, BCA, integrated BCA-MCA, AI/Data Science UG+PG), and most Arts/Science/Social Science UG+PG programmes across Amritapuri, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kochi, Mysuru, Nagercoil, Amaravati, Haridwar and Faridabad campuses.
