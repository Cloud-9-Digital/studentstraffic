## Resolution pass 2026-10-25 (owner claude-tn-dsisr-20261025)

The missing primary declaration was found and read; the status stays `held` for a different,
narrower reason. Full memo and evidence:
`research/india-source-bundles/dsisr-resolution-20261025/` (`findings.md`, `evidence.json`,
`fetch-log.json`).

**Identity now settled.** Department of Higher Education notification **No. 9-13/2026-U.3(A) dated
24 August 2026** accords approval for conferment of Institution deemed to be University status under
distinct category to Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Institute of Science and Research, NH-45
Trichy-Chennai National Highways, Siruvachur, Perambalur, formed by amalgamation of Dhanalakshmi
Srinivasan Medical College and Hospital, Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Dental College and Srinivasan
College of Nursing. The department's public order page returned HTTP 504; the record and PDF were
read through the same department's CMS JSON API and its static upload host.

**No 2026-27 award is made in the DSISR name.** The notification post-dates AY 2026-27 admissions.
NMC's AY 2026-27 register keeps all Siruvachur medical rows (MBBS 250 seats plus every MD/MS/DM/M.Ch.
row) under The Tamilnadu Dr. MGR Medical University with annual renewal granted and **no
affiliation-change note** — while the same register does print such a note for the Thuraiyur Road
college. The dental college's live BDS 2026-2027 admission card and the nursing college's site both
name Dr. M.G.R. Medical University. The university's own site remains a single page: three existing
colleges linked out, four *proposed* schools, no prospectus, fee page or application route.

**Blocking conflict (why no payload was drafted).** NMC rows for college TN/010/P/3 (Dhanalakshmi
Srinivasan Institute of Medical Sciences and Hospital, Thuraiyur Road) name
"Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Institute of Science and Research (Deemed to be University)" in the
structured university field, but the remark on the same rows says the college is "Affiliated to
Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Institute of Research and Technology (Deemed to be University) from AY
2026-27". DSISR (notified 24.08.2026, three Siruvachur health institutions) and DSIRT (notified
01.06.2026 over Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Arts & Science for Women) are distinct bodies, and
**neither notification lists the Thuraiyur Road medical college as a constituent**. The 23 rows
therefore cannot be attached to either catalogue university without merging two institutions on a
self-contradictory regulator field.

**Hold-breakers:** a UGC/DoHE instrument or corrected NMC row placing the Thuraiyur Road college in
DSISR; or a DSISR prospectus/fee/application set for AY 2027-28; or an NMC/NDC/INC 2027-28 record
naming DSISR for a Siruvachur programme.

All 62 inventory rows keep `held-awarding-transition`: the reason is unchanged in kind (no verified
DSISR-made current award), only the evidence is now primary and the gap is specific. No fee,
programme, intake or language claim is made for DSISR, nothing is transferred from DSIRT (0290), DSU
(0279) or DS College of Engineering (0148), and no reservation, media, upload, database write or
