## Notes on methodology and conflicts to resolve before publishing
- The BDS row is marked open_to_international on the strength of the international fee schedule (USD 18,000/year, NEET-based), but the existing bundle payload's `thingsToConsider` states the 2026 BDS fee notice lists all 100 seats as All India management seats with **no NRI category**. This is a direct conflict between two official-adjacent JSS AHER documents of different vintages; treat BDS as unresolved and re-verify against the current-year BDS admission notice before publishing rather than trusting either source alone.
- Duration was populated from the fee-schedule text where legible; pdftotext's column layout for the international fee PDF is imperfect (multi-column tables merge unpredictably), so durations and fee figures in that PDF should be re-verified against a page-by-page visual read before use in published content — this pass used it only to confirm programme existence and international-eligibility scope, not fee amounts.
- Campus: all rows are assumed Mysuru except the already-published Ooty B.Pharm; whether any of the diploma/D.Pharm/Pharm.D/M.Pharm rows also run at the Ooty pharmacy college was not confirmed this pass.
