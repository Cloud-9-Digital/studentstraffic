## Fee pass 2026-10-26

Scope: the 8 programmes still `on_request` with no `reportedFeeQuote` in bundle 0270. They are BSc Mathematics, BSc Physics, BSc Chemistry, MA English, MSc Mathematics, MSc Physics, MSc Chemistry and MSc Computer Science. Result: no fee added and no correction bundle.

Searched:

The 2025 prospectus (`prist_pros.pdf`) has no fee table for them. A site search of prist.ac.in found no other 2025–26 or 2026–27 fee notice.

  - CollegeDekho Thanjavur BSc Mathematics and BSc Chemistry course pages, updated 28 Mar 2024;

Why every Grade C figure was rejected, both because sources conflict and because the figures are implausible outliers:
- **BSc Mathematics.** CollegeBatch gives INR 9,000 per year and Collegedunia INR 9,000 for the first year. CampusOption gives INR 10,000 per year, or 30,000 or 40,000 in total. CollegeDekho gives INR 20,000 in total (6,666 per year) on one page and INR 20,000 annual on another.
- **BSc Physics and Chemistry.** Figures range from INR 6,666 to 20,000 per year across the same sites, and CollegeDekho contradicts itself between its total and annual labels.
- **MA English.** Figures are INR 12,500 per year (CollegeBatch), 10,000 per year (CampusOption) and 12,000 per year (CollegeDekho, a programme-level figure that isn't English-specific).
- **MSc Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Computer Science.** CollegeBatch gives INR 10,000 to 20,000 per year by subject. CampusOption gives about INR 14,000 per year, grouped. CollegeDekho gives INR 24,000 in total or 30,000 per year, grouped. Collegedunia gives INR 28,000, grouped.
- **Staleness check.** For sibling programmes that do have an official 2026–27 rate, the same aggregators are far below it. Official BSc Computer Science, BA English and the three biology MSc programmes are each INR 15,000 per semester, which is INR 30,000 a year. For BSc Computer Science the aggregators show INR 10,000 to 26,000 per year, and for MBA INR 50,000 per year against an official 60,000. So the aggregator figures predate the current tariff. Publishing them as "Approx. fees" would understate the likely current fee about threefold.
- **Not substituted.** No sibling-programme or other-campus rate was used in place of a missing figure. The 8 programmes stay `on_request`.
