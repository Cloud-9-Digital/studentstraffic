                                     DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The Proctor

      Reports to the Registrar and the Vice-Chancellor

      To take care of all aspects of the University Hostels, to ensure comfortable stay for students, besides
       the safety and welfare of the inmates.

      Responsible for ensuring utmost discipline among the inmates of the Hostel and also recommending
       penal action against any erring student which includes disobeying the Rules and Regulations of the
       Hostels and Students Conduct Rules.


 Duty Officers

      Reports to Proctor - DSW
 •     Fall in for morning colours, PT etc.,
 •     Fall in for sick parade.
 •     Fall in for special leave party.
 •     Outgoing and incoming routines of cadets proceeding on leave.
 •     Attend to incoming calls in the absence of receptionist.
 •     Maintain discipline in all academic areas during class hours.
 •     Attending to all calls, giving information regarding courses and connecting the calls to concerned
       extension numbers.
 •     Monitoring the cadets for parade /colours and carrying out inspections as required.
 •     Monitoring discipline in dining hall during Breakfast, lunch and dinner (duly assisted by ACCs).
 •     Make preparations for parade.
      Checking the movements of cadets in /out proceeding on special leave or to hospital for treatment.
 •     Updating the notice board, maintaining in / out register, collecting couriers / mails / letters etc.,
       addressed to AMET and cadets.
 •     Mustering the grounded cadets from time to time during off hours and controlling "shore leave" of
       cadets.
 •     Taking surprise rounds at times.
 •     Informing the Proctor/Registrar if any untoward incidents take place in off hours.
 •     Custodian of keys of all rooms.
 •     Submitting the Movement Register to Advisor – Security, Vigilance and Safety.
 •     Maintaining log book on daily basis.
 •     Responsible for handling emergency sick cases, if any.

                                RULES FOR HOSTEL CADETS/STUDENTS

GENERAL.
 The proctor is empowered to deal all the issues by themselves and take considerable actions.
 Parents will be intimated about their wards absence via SMS on the same day.
 Anybody found troubling the warden or the security will be dealt seriously.
 All the above said rules are a standing order from the Vice Chancellor and doesn’t require any
   enquiry in case of violation.
 All the above said rules are to be followed religiously without excuses.
 AMET University provides hostel facilities for cadets of DG approved courses and other students.
    They will be admitted to the hostel as per roll number.
   Cadets are advised to take care of their personal belongings and valuable things. The Hostel
    administration will not be responsible for any loss.
   Cadets are instructed to co – operate with the hostel Wardens / staff for the amicable situation.
   Nobody is allowed to possess any vehicle or the related materials inside the campus.
   Cadets are not permitted to go out (Short Shore Leave) without permission from the Principal/ Dean/
    DSW/Proctor.
   Gate pass will be issued after the approval of the Proctor/DSW and Cadets should make entry in the
    IN and OUT register which will be placed in the Duty Officer Cabin and also Hostel gate.
   Cadets can utilize the Television facilities, but it should be switched off before 2300 hrs.
   Cadets found consuming alcohol or any other unwanted substance shall be severely dealt with and
    terminated from the campus immediately.
   If found, any cadets who involved in violation or indiscipline, Hostel administration will take
    disciplinary action against such cadets.
   If any issues pertaining to maintenances, Cadets can register their issues in the maintenance register,
    which is placed in every Hostel at Security cabin.


    HOSTEL TIMING.
          All the entries to the hostel will be closed by 0855hrs and will not be opened under any
           circumstances before 1600hrs.
          Cadets are strictly ordered to attend their classes. Spend time in the library if no classes.
          The hostel gate will be closed by 2100hrs & it will be opened only the next day by 0500hrs.
          Any late comers will not be entertained & he/she will not be allowed in, besides marking
           them absent.




                                                                                                           19
    HOSTEL ATTENDANCE.
          Everyone should remain in their respective rooms (strictly) during attendance i.e. by 2100hrs.
          If a cadet/student is found missing in his/her room, he/she will be marked absent (Bell will be
           rung to indicate the cadets/students that they are to be in their rooms for attendance/Study
           hours.


    ABSENTEE
        Anybody found missing from the campus without proper permission will be marked absent
         and he/she will subsequently be suspended for 15 days.
        Even when a cadet/student comes late at night (though he is academically present), he will be
         marked absent for that day besides suspension.

  SHORE LEAVE.
 The hostel timings are to be adhered even on Sundays & shore leaves. (no excuses like traffic)
 Also cadets/students are not entitled to extend their shore leaves (Diwali, Pongal) on their own for
   their convenience. This is applicable to all the cadets/students irrespective of their locality. They will
   be marked absent with the punishment as said earlier.
 Absentees on Sundays muster will also be dealt seriously and Suspension for 15 days.
 Any cadet/student who wishes to attend sibling’s marriage function, they have to get permission
   from the Dean with parents.

    ALCOHOL/WEED.
   Anybody found under the influence of alcohol/weed is to be terminated without further enquiry &
    delay, irrespective of whether you are caught red handed or not.
   Anybody suspected to have such material can be taken up for investigation without any delay.

    HOUSE KEEPING
    Student rooms and hostel premises
   Cadets / Students themselves keep their rooms neat and clean.
   No waste materials are to be thrown on the hostel floor. No waste is to be thrown out of the
    window.
   Clothes are to be dried strictly on the clothing lines only, provided in the corridors.
   Hygiene in the hostel bathrooms and toilets are to be properly maintained by cleaning staff.
   Bathrooms and Corridors are cleaned by the house keeping staff regularly.


    VEHICLES.
   Nobody is allowed to possess any vehicles or the related materials inside the campus.

                                DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE – PROCEDURE


  Step - 1 : Proctor /DSW will receive disciplinary Complaint / Issue from affected Students or get
     information from Residential Wardens / Security – Advisor / Other sources.
  Step - 2 : Proctor /DSW will conduct the preliminary enquiry with affected students / Involved
     Students and get a statement from both side.
  Step - 3 : Proctor /DSW will submit/ forward this statement with remarks to the Registrar.
  Step - 4 : The Registrar will intimate to the Dean – Student Welfare for convene the disciplinary
     committee meetings.
  Step - 5 : Disciplinary committee meeting (Committee list encl.) will enquire the Complaints /Issues
     elaborately and submit the recommendation to the Registrar.
  Step - 6 : The Registrar will implement the decision through circular as per recommendation by the
     disciplinary committee.
  Step - 7 : The AR-Student Affairs will circulate the order to the concern and get acknowledgement and
     send a message to the concern Students’ parents.

                         POLICY REGARDING ANTI-RAGGING
Ragging in any form in the hostel is illegal. The University is determined to prohibit, prevent and eliminate
the scourge of ragging. The Students can approach the anti–ragging committee for reporting ragging related
matters.
"Ragging is prohibited as per the decision of the Supreme Court of India” and guidelines issue kept every
now and then.

RAGGING BANNED

The University views ragging very seriously. The University is determined to prohibit, prevent and eliminate
the scourge of ragging including any conduct whether by spoken words or written or by an act which has the
effect of teasing or treating with rudeness a fresher student or indulging in indiscipline activities or which is
likely to cause annoyance, hardship or psychological harm in any fresher or any other student are asking any
student to do any act such student will not in the ordinary course does and which as the effect of causing or
generating a sense of shame or torment or embarrassment so as adversely affect the physique or psyche of
such fresher student with or without an intent to derive a sadistic pleasure or showing off
power/authority/superiority anywhere in the campus and thereby to provide a healthy physical and
psychological development of all students.

The University campus encompasses all its departments' constituent units and all the premises, whether
having academic, residential, playground, canteen or other such premises of the University, whether located
within the campus or outside and to all means of transportation of students whether public or private,
accessed by students for the pursuit of studies in the University.

Ragging constitutes one or more of the any of the following acts.

          Any conduct by any student or students whether by words spoken or written or by an act which
           as the effect of teasing, treating or handling with rudeness fresher/student.
          Indulging in indiscipline activities by any student or students which causes or is likely to cause
           annoyance, hardship, physical or psychological harm or to raise fear or apprehension thereof in
           any fresher/student.
          Asking any student to do any act which such student will not in the ordinary course does and
           which as the effect of causing or generating a sense of shame or torment or embarrassment so as
           advisedly affect the physique or psyche of such fresher/student.
          Any act by a senior student that prevents, disrupts or disturbs the regular academic activity of
           any student/fresher.
          Exploiting the services of a fresher/student for completing the academic tasks assigned to an
           individual or a group of students.
          Any act of financial extortion or forceful expenditure burden put on a fresher/student by
           students.
          Any act of physical abuse including all variants of it; Sexual abuse, homosexual assaults, striping,
           forcing obscene and lewd acts, gestures, causing bodily harm or any other danger to health or
           person.




                                                                                                              30
       Any act or abuse by spoken words, emails, post, and public insults which would also include
        deriving perverted pleasure, vicarious or sadistic thrill from actively or passively participating in
        the discomfiture to fresher/student.
  The students can approach the anti-ragging committee for reporting ragging related matters. The
action for ragging will include in addition to suspension, expulsion of the guilty from the University and
lodging of F.I.R with the police.

                                         Procedures to be followed in case of fire
            Raise Alarm (Siren)
            Safety Officer/Duty Officer to announce evacuation by Public Addresser system
            Duty officer to call Local Fire Station Phone No.: 101 (or) 044-28554309/ 28554311
            Students and staff to assemble in the parade ground.
            Carry out a head count to identify missing person.
            Identify the type of fire and use the appropriate fire extinguishers
            Use portable handheld fire extinguishers to fight small fire.
            If Fire becomes big and uncontrollable, Rig two fire hoses immediately
            Open appropriate valves and hydrants and Start Fire Pump. Isolate Electric supply
            Direct Jet/Spray of water towards fire
            Extinguish fire by using water from 40,000 ltr. Overhead tank
            Once fire brigade arrives from local fire station work with them to fight the fire successfully

         Do boundary cooling to prevent the spreading of fire.
         Remove combustible material close by to area under fire.
         Resident doctor to be standby to administer first aid to injured personal.
         Ambulance to be standby to transport injured personal to Chettinad Multi speciality hospital.
         After extinguishing the fire assess the damages
         Take a head count to ascertain any missing person
         Intimate the higher official about the extent of damages
         Probe the root cause for fire and report to the Higher Authorities.
         Conduct safety awareness meeting and Counsel the erring individual.


ELECTRICAL SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

    Check electrical appliances and cords / plugs regularly and disconnect appliances (where practical),

      when not in use.

    Safety must be ensured at all times.

    Electrical connections should have proper earthing.

    Check emergency lighting.
