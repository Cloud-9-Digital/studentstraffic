Department Overview

         The Department of English at Periyar University is committed to foster a vibrant intellectual environment
aligned with the university‟s vision of academic excellence and social responsibility. The department promotes
critical inquiry, creative expression, and scholarly engagement through the study of English literature and related
disciplines.

      The M.A. English programme encourages students to engage deeply with literary texts, theoretical
frameworks, and diverse cultural contexts. It emphasises the development of analytical skills, interpretative

                                                                                                           3
abilities, and independent thinking, enabling students to critically examine historical, social, and cultural
dimensions of literature.

       The curriculum is designed to provide a strong foundation in core areas such as British, American, Indian,
and postcolonial literatures, along with exposure to contemporary literary theories and interdisciplinary approaches.
The programme also incorporates components that enhance communication skills, research aptitude, and academic
writing.

        In addition to classroom learning, the department actively organises seminars, workshops, literary activities,
and student-led initiatives to support holistic development. These academic and co-curricular engagements aim to
prepare students for higher studies, research, teaching, and various career opportunities in academia, media, and
other professional fields.

       The department also offers value-added and skill-oriented courses to enhance the employability and
professional competence of its graduates.

Vision of the Department of English

       To create an intellectually vibrant environment for the study of English that combines critical thinking,
research excellence and humanistic values, shaping scholars who are both analytically proficient and socially
conscious.

REGULATIONS
Conditions for Admission

       Candidates who have successfully completed a B.A. in English or any other Bachelor‟s degree from this
University or from any other University recognized as equivalent by the Syndicate are eligible to apply for
admission to the M.A. English programme (Two-Year Programme). Preference will be given to candidates with a
B.A. in English. Candidates from other disciplines must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in Part II English
and should have studied at least two papers in English under the non-semester system or four papers in English
under the semester system.

Eligibility for the Award of Degree

        A candidate shall be eligible for the award of the M.A. English degree upon successful completion of the
prescribed programme of study in the University Department, Periyar University, Salem–11, for a minimum period
of two academic years. The candidate must have passed all examinations across the four semesters, secured a
minimum of 50% aggregate marks, and fulfilled all other requirements as prescribed by the University.




                                                                                                            5
Duration of the Programme

        The Master of Arts (M.A.) in English is a two-year postgraduate programme comprising four semesters. The
programme follows the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) with Continuous Internal Assessment. Each academic
year is divided into two semesters:

      First and Third Semesters: July to November
      Second and Fourth Semesters: December to April

Research Requirements

        Research is an important part of the programme. Students are encouraged to develop a research mindset
through seminar presentations and academic publications. These activities also help in preparing their final
dissertation/project in the fourth semester. In the first year, students should participate and present atleast one paper
at a national or international seminars. In the second year, students are expected to publish atleast one paper in a
recognised peer -reviewed journal or chapter in an edited book with ISSN/ISBN number.

Examination

        The programme consists of four semester examinations. The First Semester examination will be conducted
in the middle of the first academic year, and the Second Semester examination at the end of the first academic year.
Similarly, the Third Semester examination will be conducted in the middle of the second academic year, and the
Fourth Semester examination at the end of the second academic year.

Scheme of Examination

The scheme of examination for each semester is as follows:

Theory Papers

      External Examination: 75 Marks
      Internal Assessment: 25 Marks
      Total: 100 Marks
      Duration: 3 Hours

Internal Assessment Components

Internal marks (25) shall be awarded based on the following components:

      Best two tests out of three: 10 Marks
      Assignment: 5 Marks
      Seminar/Presentation: 5 Marks
      Attendance: 5 Marks
      Total: 25 Marks

                                                                                                              6
Project Evaluation

Project Work

      Guide Evaluation: 25 Marks
      External Examiner Evaluation: 25 Marks
      Total: 50 Marks

Viva Voce

      Guide: 25 Marks
      External Examiner: 25 Marks
      Total: 50 Marks

Total (Project + Viva Voce): 100 Marks

Scheme of Evaluation

       Evaluation is carried out on a continuous basis throughout the course. There shall be four components of
evaluation during each semester. The first internal assessment will be conducted in the 7th week, the second in the
11th week, and the third in the 16th week. The End Semester Examination will be held in the 19th week.

       Internal assessments may include objective- type questions, short analytical/critical responses, essays, or a
combination of these methods. The End Semester Examination shall be a University-conducted theory examination
following the prescribed question paper pattern.

Requirement for Proceeding to Subsequent Semester

   1. Candidates must register for the First Semester Examinations immediately after admission into the
      Postgraduate Programme.
   2. Candidates are permitted to progress from the First Semester to the Final Semester regardless of failure in
      any semester examinations. However, they must register for all arrear subjects from previous semesters
      along with the subjects of the current (subsequent) semester.
   3. Eligibility to proceed to the next semester is subject to fulfilling the minimum attendance requirements as
      prescribed by the Syndicate of Periyar University from time to time.

Passing Minimum

      A candidate shall be declared to have passed in an individual paper if he/she secures not less than 50% of
       the prescribed marks in the examination.
      A candidate shall be declared to have passed the entire programme only upon successfully passing all
       prescribed papers in accordance with the scheme of examinations and earning a total of 103 credits.
      Candidates who fail to secure the minimum required marks in any paper(s) must reappear and pass the same
       in subsequent examinations.

Classification of Successful Candidate

      Candidates who secure 60% or more in aggregate shall be declared to have passed in First Class.
      Candidates who secure less than 60% but pass all papers shall be declared to have passed in Second
       Class.
      Candidates who secure 75% or more in aggregate shall be awarded First Class with Distinction, provided
       they pass all examinations prescribed for the course in their first appearance.

Project      Viva-Voce 50 marks                           Dissertation 50 marks
