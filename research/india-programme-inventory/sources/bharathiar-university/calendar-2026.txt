                                  Vision and Mission


Vision

         Bestow globally comparable quality education on youth, embodied with
character building, to invoke the University's motto “Educate to Elevate” and uphold
the secular ideals of the nation.
                                -as envisioned by Mahakavi Subramania Bharathiar


Mission

     To be innovative and inclusive, committed to excelling in teaching, research and
knowledge transfer and to serving the social, cultural and economic needs of the
nation.
      To innovate and offer educational programmes in various disciplines through
synergistic interaction with the industry and society.
      To impart knowledge and skills and to provide students with a learning
environment to acquire attitudes and equip them to face the emerging challenges of the
knowledge era.
    To provide equal opportunity to women students, differently abled, and minorities
and prepare them to be equal partners in accomplishing the scientiﬁc and technological
demands of the nation.
      To contribute to the advancement of knowledge through applied research leading
to newer products and processes.
       To prepare the students to work for societal transformation with a commitment
to justice and equality and emerge as job providers.
      To inculcate in students a global vision with skills of international competence.

                               1. About the University


         Bharathiar University named after the great visionary and revolutionary
National poet Subramania Bharathi, was established in Coimbatore at the foothills of
Maruthamalai by the Government of Tamil Nadu in February 1982, with its
jurisdiction over the districts of Coimbatore, Erode, Tirupur and Nilgiris. This
University is the offspring of the world renowned University of Madras.

        The University has its principles enshrined in the lofty motto “Educate to
Elevate”. Every effort is harnessed to realize the dreams of the poet of making
educational institutions the temples of learning. It is the aim of the University to
participate in the task of inculcating necessary knowledge, skills, creative attitudes and
values among the youth of this country, to contribute more effectively towards
establishing an equitable social and economic order for the secular ideals of our
Nation.

         It is located in a sprawling area of about 1000 acres, free from pollution, 12
kms away from Coimbatore city. The atmosphere is quiet, serene and ideal for
intellectual pursuits. The campus has a number of well-designed buildings which
house various teaching and research schools, library, administrative ofﬁce, and hostels.

       The University is an afﬁliating type, recognized by the University Grants
Commission (UGC). A member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and
Commonwealth Universities. Bharathiar University oversees 143 afﬁliated colleges
which include Government Colleges (20), Aided colleges (16), Self -Financing
Colleges (93) and stand -alone institutions of M.B.A and M.C.A (13).

        The University has 39 departments on the campus, dedicated to teaching and
research, including 13 Schools and a School of Distance Education. All Schools /
Departments are manned by well qualiﬁed and experienced faculty members.

       Over the last few years, the University has implemented various innovative
programmes in niche areas involving industrial collaboration with an eye on the future
technological developments.

 40. International Degree Programmes offered (BU-CIA)



         The B.Sc. (Blended) Physics, B.Sc. (Blended) Biosciences and B.Sc. (Blended)
Chemistry (3 Year UG – Regular) is a collaborative effort between Bharathiar University and the
University of Melbourne, offering a content-rich and internationally recognized Bachelor's
degree in Science that emphasizes clear teaching objectives and learning outcomes. The
program integrates the four major disciplines of Science - Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and
Biology - into a common curriculum for the ﬁrst two years, followed by specialized instruction in
either Chemistry, Biosciences or Physics in the third year.

The program aims to provide students with an excellent academic foundation that will enable
them to pursue higher education both nationally and internationally. The degree will be awarded
by Bharathiar University, and a quality assurance certiﬁcate will be provided by the University of
Melbourne, Australia.

              Students’ Club Activities play an important role in promoting the holistic development of
   students by providing opportunities to explore their interests, develop skills, and enhance creativity,
   leadership, teamwork, communication, and social responsibility. The University offers a range of
   student-centric clubs catering to diverse interests and talents.The Fine Arts Club encourages students
   to showcase their creativity through visual arts, music, dance, humour, and acting. The Health Club
   promotes physical and mental well-being through yoga, awareness campaigns, and workshops. The
   Human Rights & Consumer Awareness Club creates awareness about human rights, social justice,
   civic responsibilities, and ethical consumer practices.
              The Literature & Orators Club enhances literary interests and communication skills
   through poetry readings, debates, competitions, and public-speaking activities. The Nature Club
   promotes environmental awareness through trekking, herbal gardening, and conservation activities.
   The Skill Development Club provides practical training in areas such as driving, photography, crafts,
   and cooking.The Sports Club encourages physical ﬁtness, teamwork, discipline, and leadership
   through tournaments and training programmes. The Standards Club provides opportunities to learn
   about quality, standards, and standardization through student-centric activities.Membership is open
   to all students, ensuring an inclusive environment that encourages participation, skill development,
   creativity, and meaningful peer interaction.

                       17. Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

         Bharathiar University PG programmes have core, elective and supportive
courses/papers with credits ranging from 2 to 5 depending on the lecture hours per week. Core
paper is exclusively offered in the parent department, the supportive papers are offered by
other departments and the elective papers by the parent/other departments as well. All these
courses/papers may/may not have practical training modules. Apart from this, Bharathiar
University has introduced value added courses and job oriented certiﬁcate courses in all the
programs to improve the students’ skill sets.

         Online learning opportunities in Health and Wellness and Disaster Management,
enabling students to acquire essential knowledge and life skills beyond their core academic
discipline. These courses contribute to the holistic development of students by promoting
physical and mental well-being, preparedness, safety awareness, resilience, and responsible
citizenship.

• The minimum and maximum periods for the completion of a programme are given below:

                  4 Semseters (Minimum) and 8 Semseters (Maximum)

• The minimum number of credits 90+2 to be earned for the successful completion of the 2
  year PG programmes, which shall include a minimum number of 6 credits from the
  supportive courses and 12 from the elective courses. At least one elective/supportive course
  must be opted for in the ﬁrst three semesters.

• The remaining 72 credits are allotted to core papers/courses offered by the parent
  departments.
                                                                          th
• This includes theory, practical and project. The project will be in the 4 semester. As a special
  case, 72 credits may also include internship/ﬁeld trip/industrial visit.

• The MBA Programme, which comes under AICTE norms, a minimum of 128 credits are
  needed for completion. Keeping the diverse input of candidates in MBA, all elective courses
  are offered only in the III & IV semesters. Thus, core paper alone will be taught in the I and II
  semesters of MBA.

• All the students are required to complete online courses offered by SWAYAM, MOOCS and
  NPTEL in order to earn the compulsory extra credits before the end of the 3rd semester.

         Outcome Based Education (OBE) is followed in the University. OBE is a student-
centric teaching and learning methodology in which the course delivery and assessment is
designed to achieve the stated objectives and outcomes.

  Semester-wise Registration

        The student has to register for the opted supportive/elective courses/papers with the
respective faculty, after due announcement of the prescribed dates and the
beginning/commencement of each semester.

         On joining the University, each student is assigned to a faculty advisor. The student
can register for course(s) which he/she intends to take during a given semester on the basis of
the programme for each discipline as given in the course of the study bulletin and as per the
advice given by his/her faculty adviser. The faculty adviser is expected to discuss with the
student and then decide on the nature of the courses for which the student can register during
the semester within the framework of the CBCS guidelines.
Procedure for Registration
         The registration schedule is announced in advance by the Head of the Department.
Students should contact the respective faculty advisor of their department for guidance and
registration. The student advisor on the instructions of HOD can suggest possible
elective/supportive course(s) for registration by the candidate. In any case, registration must be
completed on or before the prescribed last date for registration. Students having outstanding fee
dues to the institute or a hostel, shall not be permitted to register. Late registration may be
permitted for certain period only due to a valid reason and on payment of a late registration fee
prescribed from time to time by CBCS ofﬁce. However, if a student fails to register during any
semester, his/her studentship is liable to be cancelled.

                              25. Free Education Scheme



         Bharathiar University has implemented Free Education Scheme for poor and
talented students from the academic year 2007-2008. Students desirous of joining various-
graduate courses can approach the afﬁliated colleges for admission. The colleges have been
given 5% extra seats in each course for admitting talented and poor students under this
scheme.

The University is implementing the scheme to ensure opportunities for the talented poor
students in the Post-graduate courses offered in the University Departments and students
can approach their respective Heads of the Departments for admission under this
schemee.

Eligibility
Merit-cum-economic status is the main criterion for the selection of the students.
Preference will be given to those students who come under the following categories.

Parental Status : Orphan, Destitute, Single Parent, Son or Daughter of divorcees,
                  Children supported by NGOs.
Social Status   : Community & Rural/Urban.
Economic Status : Income of the parent(s)/ guardian, property/assets of the family.
Academic Status : Marks in S.S.L.C., H.S.C., Under-Graduate Degree programme with
                  consistent performance and extracurricular activities.

The prescribed application can be downloaded from the University website and the ﬁlled-in
application be sent to the respective Principal of the afﬁliated college for admission in the
college and the Head of the Department concerned for admission in the University
Department, on or before the due date.

Those who are already admitted under this scheme in any one of the afﬁliated colleges of
Bharathiar University are not eligible to apply to the courses offered in the University
Departments.

A separate counselling will be conducted to select the students under this scheme.

                   30. Quota for Sports and Physically Challanged


        One seat is reserved for permanently disabled candidates and one seat is reserved
for sports person for admission into each of the programmes offered by the University
Departments.

          Candidates seeking admission under physically challenged quota shall produce a
Medical Certiﬁcate on his/her permanent physical disability by an orthopaedic surgeon who
is in the rank of an Assistant Surgeon. The candidates under this category will be selected
on the basis of his/her score in Tamilnadu Common Entrance Test and such candidates
should physically be able to undergo the course.

• Candidates seeking admission under sports quota will be selected on the basis of their
  performance/representation at the following levels:articipation at the international level,
  the candidate should have been sponsored by a National organization.

• Participation at the National level, the candidate should have been sponsored by a State
  Organisation.

• Participation at the inter-University level, the candidate should have been sponsored by a
  Zonal or District Association.

• Participation at the State Level, the candidate should have been sponsored by a Zonal or
  District Association.

           The performance of the candidates in sports that are included in the All India inter-
University Competitions alone will be considered. Sports like Riﬂe Shooting, Karate, Judo
etc, will not be considered. Students seeking admission under sports quota should be below
25 years of age. The achievement of the candidates in sports in their UG course of study
alone will be considered for selection.
