         Madurai Kamaraj University established in 1966 has been accredited with
grade A++ in the 4th Cycle (2021) by NAAC. In the survey of Government
Multidisciplinary Universities Rankings (2022) by Education World India,
University stood at 9th ranking at All India level and first among the
Universities in Tamil Nadu.

         Madurai Kamaraj University is, with the social concern of catering to the
educational needs of rural students in and around Madurai district and also with
the goal of empowering the rural women by disseminating knowledge.

V. SELECTION PROCEDURE

   1. For the P.G Programme (Entrance based), the selection of candidate is
      based on the 50% weightage marks in the Entrance Examination
      conducted by Madurai Kamaraj University and 50% weightage marks on
      UG marks up to 5th semester. For Biotechnology Programme, the
      selection is purely (100%) based on entrance examination marks only.
   2. For the P.G Programme (Non-Entrance), the selection of the candidate
      is based on the UG marks obtained up to V semester/II year
      (Non-Semester) of the UG Programme even though the candidate has
      already passed the degree.
   3. For the M.B.A. Programme there will be an interview and group
      discussion following the entrance examination results.
   4. For M.P.Ed. Programme, in addition to the Entrance Examination,
      Practical Examination and Viva-voce will be conducted after the
      Entrance Test and date will be intimated later by the department. For
                                                                             11
       Practical Examination, candidates should report in sports uniform, shoes
       and other personal sports kits like Hockey stick, Racquets etc. The test
       process is likely to be held the whole day.
   5. For the Integrated Programme, Certificate, Diploma, Higher Diploma,
      P.G. Diploma Programme there is no entrance examination. Selection is
      based on the marks obtained in the concerned course / subjects.
