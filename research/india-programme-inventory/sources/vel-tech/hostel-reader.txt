# Hostels

### About

Vel Tech University provides safe, comfortable, and student-friendly residential facilities that create a home away from home. Our hostels are designed to support students’ academic focus, personal growth, and overall well-being by offering a secure environment, modern amenities, and a vibrant campus life.

With separate hostels for boys and girls, the university ensures disciplined living, hygienic surroundings, and 24/7 support services within the campus.
