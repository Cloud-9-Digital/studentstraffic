## **NRI candidates seeking admission should fulfill the minimum eligibility criteria mentioned below:**

### **Eligibility Criteria for B.Tech. Programmes**

**Eligibility criteria for an NRI candidate:**

**For B.Tech programmes:**

*   Candidates should have completed 12 years of schooling (10+2 years equivalent to Indian Education system).
*   Minimum average of 50% in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry (MPC) in standard XII or equivalent (Grade ‘C’).
*   MPC aggregate is taken for admission in to B.Tech programmes. MPC aggregate is taken for admission in to B.Tech in Biomedical and Biotechnology programmes.
*   Candidates should have cleared English as one of the subjects in the qualifying examination

**Certificates to be produced at the time of admission**

*   Mark sheets/ grade card (certificate).
*   In case, the candidate has opted for Physical Science (Physics Chemistry), the candidate should produce approval certificate from the school and from AIU/Board of Studies.
*   Employer Certificate from the sponsor.( Parent Only )
*   Valid passport and Visa copies.

**Sponsorship:**

*   For NRI candidates the sponsorship should be given by either of the parent and not by relative/friend.

**Eligibility criteria for an NRI candidate:**

*   Mathematics and Physics at A level (or equivalent) are mandatory.
*   Candidates should have opted for English as a medium of Instruction.
*   The third subject can be Chemistry/ Computer Science/ Electronics/ Biotechnology/ Biology for pursuing B.Tech.
*   For Biomedical / Biotechnology, the eligible combination is MPC (Mathematics Physics Chemistry).
*   Minimum average of 50% in standard 12 or equivalent (Grade ‘C’)
*   For candidates from Nepal, minimum of 50% aggregate in the above subjects for Class 11 and Class 12 put-together.
*   Candidate should produce grade card/ equivalent mark sheets attained from the school.
*   Proof of foreign nationality should also be produced. Eg. Passport/ National ID

[Click here to check for eligible Board of Examinations](https://www.veltech.edu.in/board-examination/)

**BOARD OF EXAMINATION :**

The following list of examinations which are considered equivalent to the 10+2 of Indian Education system

**Europe :**

*   GCE Examination of UK with Higher grades in five subjects at the Ordinary level (O level) and Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology at the Advanced level A level.
*   High School Certificate Examination, University of Cambridge Local Examinations Syndicate, UK.
*   International Baccalaureate Examination of Geneva.

**Africa:**

*   12 years Secondary Stage qualifications of National Examination Board / Council of Ministry of Education of Kenya, Nigeria, Sudan, Tanzania Uganda and Rwanda.

**Asia:**

*   12 years High School Graduation / Diploma / Certificate of an approved Board Schools of China, Thailand, Indonesia, Bhutan, Syria, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Yemen, Saudi Arabia, UAE and other Gulf Countries.
*   High School Certificate HSC and Cambridge School Certificate A level from the accredited institutions of Mauritius.

**North America :**

*   High School Graduation from accredited institutions of USA.
*   12 years High School – Grade 12 pass from the accredited institutions of Canada

Any other examination of the Foreign country as found equivalent to 10+2 examination of India by Association of Indian Universities AIU

 REGISTRATION 

All students staying in India , longer than 6 months are required to register with FRRO.  
The following documents are required at the time of registration with the Foreigners Registration Officer.  
(i) Photocopy of the passport and initial visa.  
(ii) Four photographs of the applicant.  
(iii) Details of residence in India .  
(iv) HIV test report from one of the WHO recognized institutions for people in the age group of 15 to 60 years, if the foreigner is visiting India on a visa for more than one year.  
(v) Bona-fide certificate from the University/College/Institution in case of student visa.  
(vi) Proof of a bank account/ sufficient funds, for non-scholarship students  
**Various foreign registration offices in India are:-**  
The Foreigners Regional Registration Offices (FRRO’s) are located at CHENNAI  
Foreigners Regional Registration Office  
Haddows Road , Chennai  
Ph.: 91-44-28278210  
Fax: 91-44-28240338Certificates of registration will be issued by the registration officers.Foreigners should surrender their certificates of registration to the officer of the place where they are registered or of the place where they intend to depart or to the immigration officer at the port/check post of exit from India . If the certificate is surrendered to anyone other than the immigration officer at the port/check post of exit, the foreigners should produce the receipt indicating such surrender of the document to the immigration officer at the port/check post of exit.  
Registration is required to be done only once within the stipulated period, even though you may go out of India on multiple entry facility during the validity of the visa. Only if you enter India on a new visa will you be required to register again.  
Foreigners can visit restricted /protected areas only after getting a valid permit as visa alone is not enough to visit such places.
