 Eligibility  Admission Procedure 

## M.Sc. programme

*   B.Sc. with minimum 50% in their respective domain.

## M.A. programme

#### For Domestic Students:

*   BA English with minimum 50% in their respective domain.

#### For International Students::

*   The eligibility criterion for all programmes for international applicants is minimum 50% in their qualifying examination and having studied the pre-requisite subjects for admission into the desired program.

## Direct Admission

Candidate can take admission by coming in person to the university

*   The provisionally selected candidates have to present themselves in person to complete the admission procedure.
