# School of Commerce

### Vision

The School of Commerce is dedicated to integrating all aspects of commerce to educate and train innovative challenges and competent global human resources suitable for the industry, business, and services sector.

### Mission

*   To provide students with a well-rounded education in commerce, blending theoretical knowledge with practical applications
*   To instill a strong sense of ethics and integrity, preparing students to lead responsibly in the business world.
*   To inspire innovation and entrepreneurial spirit, supporting students in launching and managing new business ventures.
*   To engage in initiatives that contribute to economic and social development, promoting the well-being of the broader community.
*   To evolve educational processes to ensure the balance between head, heart, and hand for holistic personality development.

### Core Values for the School of Commerce

*   To uphold the highest standards of ethical behavior and honesty in all academic and professional endeavors.
*   To strive for excellence in teaching, research, and service, ensuring the highest quality education and outcomes for our students.
*   To encourage teamwork and collaboration among students, faculty, and industry partners to achieve common goals.
*   To cultivate resilience and adaptability, equipping students to navigate and thrive in a rapidly changing business landscape, professionalism, valuing everyone’s contributions, and perspectives.

 Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) 

*   **PEO1:** To apply and enhance the knowledge and skills acquired, to become a creative professional in their commerce domain.
*   **PEO2:** To engage in self-directed continuous learning for global competency to promote professional and personal growth.
*   **PEO3:** To develop leadership and entrepreneurial skills by leveraging core competencies while upholding values and ethics.
*   **PEO4:** To work towards achieving economic and social equity by applying relevant knowledge.
*   **PEO5**: To enhance analytical and methodological skills necessary for conducting high-quality research and providing valuable insights into business practices.

 Programme Outcomes (POs) 

*   PO1: Integrate knowledge from various disciplines to address complex business challenges and provide comprehensive solutions.
*   PO2: Engage in continuous learning and professional development to stay abreast of emerging trends and advancements in commerce.
*   PO3: Utilize advanced theoretical and practical insights to develop innovative solutions to complex business problems.
*   PO4: Demonstrate deep knowledge and expertise in specific areas of commerce, such as finance, marketing, or international business.
*   PO5: Apply a global perspective to research and practice, understanding and addressing international business issues and trends.
*   PO6: Adhere to high ethical standards and promote responsible practices in research and professional activities, ensuring integrity and sustainability.
*   PO7: Exhibit leadership skills by mentoring emerging scholars, leading research projects, and influencing academic and professional practices.
*   PO8: Make significant contributions to the field of commerce through impactful research, influencing practices, policies, and decision-making processes.
*   PO9: Ability to conduct original, high-quality research using advanced methodologies and analytical techniques in various commerce disciplines.
*   PO10: Proficiently communicate complex research findings and theoretical concepts to both academic and professional audiences through publications, presentations, and discussions

 Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs) 

*   PSO1: Develop and implement innovative strategies and solutions that address emerging challenges and opportunities in the commerce field.
*   PSO2: Apply in-depth knowledge of finance, marketing, or management theories to solve complex business problems and drive organizational success.
*   PSO3: Analyse and interpret global commerce trends and their impact on business strategies, contributing to effective international business practices.
*   PSO4: Conduct research and professional activities adhering to ethical standards, ensuring integrity, and promoting sustainable business practices.
*   PSO5: Present complex research findings and business concepts clearly and effectively to diverse audiences, including academic peers and industry professionals.

### About the School of Commerce

Vel Tech School of Commerce offers a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce and Doctoral Degree designed to prepare students for competitiveness in the global economic environment. The program provides advanced knowledge in accountancy, blending commerce with communication skills. Students benefit from insights shared by eminent experts in various fields, who provide the latest information and knowledge about the corporate world. Additionally, the curriculum equips students with modern management and entrepreneurship skills, enabling seamless collaboration with industry and creating a strong platform to meet the dynamic challenges of the commerce sector.

### Commerce Club

The Commerce Club offers a rich array of activities that help students grow both academically and professionally. It organizes guest lectures with industry experts, providing insights into the latest trends in commerce. Workshops on financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and stock market trading offer hands-on learning experiences. The club encourages innovative thinking through business plan competitions and case study sessions. Industrial visits give students a glimpse into real-world business operations, while community outreach programs focus on promoting financial education. The annual Commerce Fest, a key highlight, features various competitive events that foster teamwork and excellence. Networking events with alumni and professionals help students build valuable industry connections. Overall, the Commerce Club creates a vibrant environment for students to develop the skills and knowledge needed to thrive in the field of commerce.
