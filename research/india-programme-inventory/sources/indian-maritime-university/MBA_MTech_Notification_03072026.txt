               INDIAN MARITIME UNIVERSITY

                    NOTIFICATION-18
          MBA & M. Tech – IMU Admissions - 2026

It is hereby notified that candidates who do not possess an IMU-
CET 2026 rank or who could not participate in the IMU
Counselling 2026 process, but are interested in pursuing MBA or
M. Tech programmes at IMU Campuses, may register through
the Google Form. The link is given below:

MBA: https://forms.gle/zzQc9Ba4pcd2AiHY9

M. Tech: https://forms.gle/pByfuRGKMg8PTJRv8

However, such candidates must have qualified in any one of the
following examinations:

For MBA:

  i.   IMU-CET 2026 / 2025 Rank
 ii.   CAT 2025 scores
iii.   MAT - May 2026 / Feb 2026 / Dec 2025 / Sep 2025 scores
iv.    CMAT – 2026 / 2025 scores
 v.    UG (Graduation) Examination

For M. Tech:

  i.   IMU-CET 2026 / 2025 Rank
 ii.   GATE 2026 / 2025 scores
iii.   CUET (PG) 2026 / 2025 scores
iv.    PGCET 2026 / 2025 scores

Candidates who register on or before the date of release of the
Second Allotment List (tentatively scheduled on 07.07.2026)
shall be considered for allotment in the Second Allotment List.
Similarly, candidates who register on or before the date of
release of the Third Allotment List (tentatively scheduled on
14.07.2026) shall be considered for allotment in the Third
Allotment List.
Candidates are requested to visit the           IMU   website
www.imu.edu.in regularly for further updates.
