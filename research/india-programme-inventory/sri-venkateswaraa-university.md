## Campus and award-scope hold

The statutory Main Campus is Ettayapuram. The university's Ordinances also name a City
Campus at 51, GNT Road, Red Hills, Chennai 600067. Every current 2026–27 programme page and
every current professional approval is at the City Campus:

- NMC Letter of Permission, 14 October 2025: MBBS intake 250 at 51 GNT Road, Nallur,
  Near Redhills Toll Plaza, Tiruvallur, Chennai 600067.
- INC notification, 25 November 2025: Basic B.Sc. Nursing of "Sri Venkateswaraa University,
  Private University, Thiruvallur, Tamil Nadu".
- PCI decision letter, 13 August 2025: D.Pharm and B.Pharm at College of Pharmacy,
  Sri Venkateswaraa University, Nallur 600067.

UGC's current private-university record for this university (record id 1357) shows no
approved off-campus centre ("--"). The UGC 2(f) letter of 5 June 2025, condition 6, prohibits
an off-campus or constituent college/unit outside the main campus without prior specific
UGC approval; condition 12 limits degree awards to the main campus. The university's
2024–25 2(f) proforma also says headquarters Ettayapuram, constituent units Nil and
off-campus centres No.

No primary source approves Redhills as the main campus or as an additional/off-campus
campus. The conflict is recorded as an evidence gap. The professional approvals are not
challenged; the UGC campus/award route is unresolved.

## Eligibility and fee evidence

- Eligibility page (`https://svuniversity.ac.in/admission/eligibility.php`): course names
  and durations only. Every "View" control is `href="#"`; no entry subjects, minimum marks or
  other prerequisites are published. This independently blocks programme-level
  `admissionsContent`.
- Fee page (`https://svuniversity.ac.in/admission/fees.php`): 2026–27 programme-wise annual
  tuition, other charges, admission fee and application fee are published. Examples:
  B.Pharm INR 1,40,000 tuition / INR 1,71,000 total per annum; D.Pharm INR 60,000 tuition /
  INR 76,000 total; MBA Hospital Management INR 1,10,000 tuition / INR 1,30,000 total. The
  page does not label the applicant audience (domestic/international/NRI), so these are not
  treated as international fees.
- MBA Hospital Management carries a published condition that admissions will be withdrawn
  from AY 2026–27 if enrolment is below 30; existing batches continue.
- D.Pharm duration differs between the eligibility page (2 years) and the fee page
  (2 years 3 months).
