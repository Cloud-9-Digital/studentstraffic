## Identity

- Official name: Sardar Raja College of Engineering (SRCE). Raja Nagar, Adaikalapattinam (Post),
  Alangulam, Tenkasi district, Tamil Nadu 627808. Older AICTE records still say Tirunelveli district,
  because Tenkasi district was carved out of Tirunelveli in 2019.
- Established in 2000. The college is self-financing and non-minority, run by the Raja Educational and
  Charitable Trust (Vadakkangulam, registration no. 711 of 1985; chairman Er. A. Jesus Raja). Its
  founder is Dr. S.A. Raja, and the college calls itself the third engineering college of the "Rajas
  group".
- It is an affiliated college, not a university. It is non-autonomous and affiliated to Anna
  University, Chennai (exam code 9527, Tirunelveli region). Anna University awards the degrees.
- AICTE permanent ID 1-2894011. AICTE type: Private-Self Financing. TNEA counselling code 4968.
- Official website: https://src.org.in. The srce.co.in address given on Wikipedia does not resolve.

## Packaging decision for `not_open` programmes

The runbook's packaging floor covers every `open_to_international` row. The runbook also allows
programmes that are unavailable to international applicants to be published for the global audience,
provided the restriction is stored and shown precisely.

These 8 programmes are packaged with `audienceEligibility.availability = "restricted"`:
- Eligible audiences are Indian nationals, plus OCI/PIO card holders who are natives of Tamil Nadu,
  via TNEA or the college's own seats.
- The page and FAQ state the restriction prominently.

Because no `open_to_international` row exists, this is the only way to publish the college. If the
reviewer decides India `not_open` programmes should not be published, hold the whole bundle.
