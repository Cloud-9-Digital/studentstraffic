## International status

The 2026-27 prospectus (instructions 9-11) admits foreign nationals including NRIs, and refugees, through
the International Centre of Madras University (ICOM), exempts them from the entrance test and reserves 15%
supernumerary seats in every PG programme. It prints separate foreign-national INR fee tables for most
degree groups, and foreign-national columns for P.G. Diploma, Diploma and Certificate courses. So every
current master's, diploma and certificate row is `open_to_international`. The B.Sc. (Blended) prospectus
(item 6) admits foreign nationals and NRIs on the same terms; it prints one fee table for all students.

## Why 85 master's degrees are held: teaching language

The schema requires a verified teaching language for each programme (`medium` cannot be a placeholder and
`instructionLanguages` needs at least one code). The 2026-27 prospectus, instructions, admission portal and
entrance schedule never state a medium of instruction, and www.unom.ac.in (department pages, regulations
and syllabi) was unreachable all day. Two programmes have source-backed languages:

- B.Sc. (Blended): "Core international curriculum delivered in English" (B.Sc. prospectus, Benefit to Students).
- M.A. Public Administration: the fee table names "Public Administration (TM & EM)", the master's list
  has a separate "M.A. Public Administration (TM)", and the entrance schedule calls that one
  "M.A. - Public Administration (TAMIL MEDIUM)". EM is read as English medium.

All other master's degrees are held with that reason in `notes`. Additional per-row blocks:

- M.A. Public Administration (Tamil medium), M.A. Tamil Literature and Culture, M.A. Tamil Studies,
  M.A. Kannada, M.A. Malayalam: `lib/catalogue-facets.ts` has no `tamil`, `kannada` or `malayalam`
  instruction-language code, so they cannot be packaged even once the language is confirmed.
- LL.M. Constitutional Law and Legal Order: the BCI recognised list could not be read.
- M.Ed.: NCTE recognition could not be checked.
- MBA and MCA: admission is through TANCET and DoTE single-window counselling (code 710; MBA 45 aided +
  50 self-supporting seats, MCA 30 seats in the 2026-27 booklet); held only for teaching language.

To release them: obtain the University's PG regulations for University Departments (or department pages)
stating the medium of instruction from www.unom.ac.in, then add each programme to the build script with
its fee group (all fee tables are already transcribed in `research-drafts/india/university-of-madras.md`).

## Constituent colleges

The scope row asks to confirm whether rows 2061/2062 are still University constituent colleges. Findings:

- The 2026-27 prospectus describes the University as 87 University Departments plus affiliated colleges and
  lists no constituent college; the admission portal and entrance schedule cover only University
  Departments and the B.Sc. (Blended).
- The University's NIRF 2026 data reports PG intake only (2-year: 1,565 sanctioned in 2024-25; 3-year: 39)
  and no UG programmes, which a constituent arts and science college would add.
- No official source names an "Engineering and Medical College" of the University.
- A third-party Q&A and Wikipedia mention a University of Madras arts and science college at Nemmeli
  (now listed as a Government college), and a college website cites G.O. No. 36 of 28.02.2019 converting
  14 university constituent colleges to Government Arts and Science Colleges from 2019-20. None of this
  could be checked on an official source (unom.ac.in, tndce.tn.gov.in and tn.gov.in unreachable).

Both rows are therefore excluded; nothing from them is packaged.
