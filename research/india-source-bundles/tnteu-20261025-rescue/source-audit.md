## Identity

- Official name: **Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University** (TNTEU). The UGC register spells the
  name "Tamil Nadu Teacher Education University"; NAAC and NCTE use "Teachers". The official site,
  admission notification and Act citation use "Teachers Education University", which is the public
  name retained here.
- Address: Gangaiamman Koil Street, Karapakkam, Chennai 600097, Tamil Nadu.
- Public state university, single-discipline (education), established by Tamil Nadu Act No. 33 of
  2008; commenced 1 July 2008 by Gazette Notification in G.O.Ms. 256, Higher Education (K2)
  Department, dated 25 June 2008 (`genesis.php`, `about_us.php`, 2026-27 brochure).
- The university is an affiliating university for teacher-education colleges in Tamil Nadu. The
  `departments.php` page states: "Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University is a Single Discipline
  (Education) University. All six Departments ... together offer one common M.Ed Programme. In
  addition ... these six Departments together offer M.Phil and Ph.D Programmes in Education."
