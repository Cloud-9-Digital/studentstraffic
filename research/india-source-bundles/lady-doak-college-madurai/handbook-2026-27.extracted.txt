                       ABOUT THE COLLEGE
 As the oldest women’s college in Madurai, Lady Doak College lays
 claim to its continuous existence for the past 77 years. Miss Katie
 Wilcox, an American missionary who had a vision and fervour for
 women’s education, founded the college on 14th July 1948 at the
 Noyes Memorial Gardens, Tallakulam, Madurai, South India, at a
 time when there was no other institution for higher education
 exclusively for women in and around the city of Madurai.

 Lady Doak College, affiliated to Madurai Kamaraj University, is one
 of the leading autonomous colleges. It is a unit of the Katie Wilcox
 Education Association, a Christian Educational Trust. As an
 ecumenical Christian college whose purpose is to impart liberal
 education to students of all creeds, this college strives to develop
 their intellectual powers, locate, identify, and cultivate their interests
 and talents and train them to be responsible and useful citizens.

 In its first year, Lady Doak College had 81 students, a few books in
 a small library room, one hostel, a few thatched sheds and a portion
 of the classroom building under construction. Since that time, the
 college has grown in every way.

 Today, we have a chapel, classrooms, lecture halls, library,
 laboratories, multimedia theatres, indoor stadium-cum- auditorium,
 open-air theatre, hostels, clinic, canteens and refreshment corners.
 Now there are 4284 students studying in the Departments of
 Humanities, Sciences, Commerce and Business Administration
 served by a committed and skilled team of about 216+ teaching and
 132+ non-teaching staff.

Academic autonomy was granted in 1978, making it one of the first
autonomous institutions in the country. A challenging curriculum
blended with relevant academic programmes, co- curricular
activities, exposure programmes and a well-developed student
support system has placed the college at the forefront in the field of
higher education. The college currently offers 26 Undergraduate, 15
Postgraduate, 8 Research Programmes, 3 PG Diploma, 3 Diploma, 9
Certificate Courses, 1 Online Diploma Course, 4 Online Certificate
Courses and 54 Value-Added Courses. Madurai Kamaraj University
has recognised the Departments of Botany, Chemistry, Economics,
English, History, Physics, Tamil and Zoology as Research Centres.
The college is also one of the first few Arts and Science Colleges in
the State of Tamil Nadu, which has initiated an International Student
Exchange programme for credit transfer.
Lady Doak College aims to bring about a wholesome transformation
of women, with a well-balanced moral, social and spiritual outlook.

                 COLLEGE ADMINISTRATION
PRINCIPAL
The Principal is the Executive Head, the Official Secretary and the
Correspondent of the college. She is also the ex-officio Secretary of the
Katie Wilcox Education Association and its Executive Board and acts as
the official representative of the Association. She appoints members of
the faculty, admits students to the various courses, residential halls and is
responsible for the maintenance of discipline and progress of the college.
She presides over all the meetings of the Senatus and of the Faculty and
Non-Teaching Staff Association. And she is the ex- officio member of
their sub-committees.

VICE-PRINCIPAL
The Vice-Principal performs the duties of the Principal in her absence
and other duties as delegated to her by the Principal. She also oversees
the functioning of the Institutional Development Officer, IQAC
Coordinators, Information Technology Coordinators, HR Coordinators,
and the Alumnae Association.

BURSAR
The Bursar provides leadership and direction to the Institution regarding
the management of finances. She advises the Finance & Accounting team
on Funds and Resource Management, Budgeting and Statutory
compliances.
She liaises with the management team on all financial matters and is
responsible for the maintenance of accounts of all financial transactions,
preparation of the Budget & conduct of audit.

DEANS OF ACADEMIC AFFAIRS
The Deans of Academic Affairs coordinate the academic work of the
Humanities and Science Departments. They are responsible for all
matters relating to the college curriculum, capacity building of faculty
through the conduct of conferences, seminars & workshops and ensuring
the maintenance of high academic quality of the Institution. They also
oversee the functioning of the Controller of Examinations, CPEC
Coordinators, Strategic Partnership Coordinator, R&D Coordinators and
Extension Activities Coordinator.

CONTROLLER OF EXAMINATIONS
The Controller of Examinations is responsible for the formative and
summative assessment of students, organising and co- ordinating the
conduct of tests/examinations, evaluation, announcement of results, issue
of marksheets and award of certificates, diplomas and degrees.

DEANS OF STUDENT AFFAIRS
The Deans of Student Affairs look after the interests of all students on
campus. They are in charge of non-resident and resident student affairs,
health services, food services, career counselling, personal counselling
services, and settlement of grievances. They carry out their work through
the Student Cabinet Members, Resident Hall Supervisor, Resident Health
Officer, Career & Personal Counsellors and Student Grievance Cell
Coordinators.

CHAPLAIN
The Chaplain is in-charge of all matters connected with the College
Chapel and organises worship programmes/ retreats/ conferences for
faculty members and students. She also co- ordinates the Student
Christian Movement activities, Christian Studies, College Choir and
Chapel Services. She carries out the work through the SCM Coordinator,
Christian Studies Coordinator, Choir Coordinator and Chapel Services
Coordinator.

CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER (COO)
The Chief Operating Officer is responsible for infrastructure and
property management, campus maintenance and ensures safety &
security of the campus. She oversees the functioning of the Campus
Manager and is also responsible for Purchases, Contracts, Agreements as
well as Centralised & Decentralised documentation.

                   ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS
   Lady Doak College follows the existing Madurai Kamaraj
   University norms for attendance and condonation.
   a.   The number of working days is fixed as 90 days/450
        hours. For all UG and PG summative examinations, a
        student who has put in 44 days attendance and less has
        to repeat the whole semester before she can proceed to
        the next semester.
   b.   A student is eligible to continue to the next semester,
        only if she fulfils a minimum of 50% attendance and has
        the following minimum Formative requirement in Part
        III Courses and makes a payment of Rs. 500/-.
   Minimum Formative requirement to continue to the next
   semester
   •    Students must have completed at least 50% of all Part
        III formative requirements for UG and at least 50% of all
        Part A formative requirements for PG.
   c.   A student who has attended classes for 68 days (75%)
        and above will be permitted to appear for the ensuing
        summative examinations without any preconditions.
   d.   Theory Courses: A student who has attended classes for
        67 days and less, but 59 days (65% to 74%) and above
        will be permitted to appear for the summative
        examination after an online payment of Rs. 300/ towards
        condonation of attendance. The list of such students can
        be accessed on the student portal.
   e.   Lab Courses: A student who has attended classes for 67
        days and less, but 59 days (65% to 74%) and above will
        be permitted to appear for the summative Lab
        examination. An online payment of Rs. 300/ towards
        condonation of attendance to be paid after the last
        working day. The list of such students can be accessed
        on the student portal.
        Students who do not have 65% of attendance on the day
        of the lab examination but have the possibility to attain
        65% in the last working day will be permitted to appear
                               42
                                              Lady Doak College

      for the Lab examination upon submitting an undertaking
      validated by the course teachers and the head of the
      department before appearing for the lab examination.
      However, if the candidate’s attendance still falls below
      65% on the last working day, her lab exam result will be
      considered NULL and VOID.
f.    A student who has attended classes for 58 days and less
      but 45 days (50% to 64%) and above cannot appear for
      summative examinations in the current semester but can
      appear for the next relevant summative examinations (as
      per college norms)
g.    For Vocational crash courses, students are required to
      have a minimum of 12 days (75%) attendance, failing
      which they will be required to redo the course in
      Semester V
h.    Half-day leave: The five hours of classes in a working
      day is divided into two sessions of 2 and 3 classes for the
      aided stream and session of 3 and 2 classes for the SF
      streams. If a student is absent for even one hour in a
      session, it will be considered as half- day leave.
i.    Leave Letters: Students are expected to submit leave
      documents on time to their respective heads of
      departments to request for or to explain their leave of
      absence from classes. Leave documents upon validation
      by the Head of the Department to be submitted at the
      Vice Principal’s office immediately.

                        LIBRARY
The J. X. Miller Memorial library offers a rich resource for
research and study with a vast collection of 1,55,016 books
and 45 periodicals. It features a general library,
departmental libraries, and specialised resource centres for
Women’s Studies and Christian Studies. The library’s
special collections on Human Rights, Environmental
Studies, International Studies, Interfaith Relations, Current
Affairs, and Competitive Examinations are its standout
features, making it a valuable asset for scholars and
students alike.
LIBRARY WORKING HOURS
 Working Days               8.00 a.m. - 6.00 p.m.
  Summer Vacation            9.00 a.m. - 5.00 p.m.
CIRCULATION SECTION
Books are issued exclusively to the staff and students of
Lady Doak College. The ID card with its barcode functions
as the Library Card. Borrowing limits are set as follows:
Undergraduate students may borrow 3 books, Postgraduate
students 4 books, and faculty members 10 books.
Reference books and periodicals are not available for
borrowing. The standard lending period is 10 days. Reserve
books are available for overnight study to all students.
Economically disadvantaged students can receive books
from the Book Bank upon recommendation from the Head
of the Department. Failure to return a book on time results
in a fine of one rupee per day. In the event of a lost book,
the borrower must either replace the book or pay three
times its cost.
SPECIAL SERVICE
Alumnae and research scholars from other institutions are
granted access to the library resources for a nominal fee of
Rs. 10 per day.
REPROGRAPHIC SECTION
The reprographic section provides users with photocopying
services.

   future careers in the Life Science research.

                       COLLEGE HYMNS
   HYMN - 1
   1. Praise, my soul, the King of heaven;
      To His feet thy tribute bring;
      Ransomed, healed, restored, forgiven,
      Who like me His praise should sing?
      Praise Him ! Praise Him ! (2)
      Praise the ever lasting king.
   2.   Praise Him for His grace and favour
        To our fathers in distress;
        Praise Him, still the same for ever,
        Slow to chide and swift to bless;
        Praise Him! Praise Him! (2)
        Glorious in His faithfulness.
   3.   Father-like He tends and spares us.
        Well our feeble frame He knows;
        In His hands He gently bears us,
        Rescues us from all our foes.
        Praise Him! Praise Him! (2)
        Widely as His mercy flows.
   4.   Frail as summer’s flower we flourish;
        Blows the wind and it is gone.
        But, while mortals rise and perish,
        God endures unchanging on;
        Praise Him! Praise Him! (2)
        Praise the high Eternal One.
   5.   Angels, help us to adore him;
        Ye behold Him face to face;
        Sun and moon,
        bow down before Him:
        Dwellers all in time and space
        Praise Him! Praise Him! (2)
        Praise with us the God of grace.

   HYMN - 2
   1. O, Master, let me walk with Thee
      In lowly paths of service free;
      Tell me Thy secret; help me bear
      The strain of toil, the fret of care.
   2. Help me the slow of heart to move
      By some clear, winning word of love;
      Teach me the way ward feet to stay,
      And guide them in the homeward way.
                               142
                                                  Lady Doak College

3.   Teach me Thy patience; still with Thee
     In closer, dearer company,
     In work that keeps faith sweet and strong,
     In trust that triumphs over wrong.
4.   In hope that sends a shining ray
     Far down the future’s broadening way,
     In peace that only Thou canst give,
     With Thee, O Master, let me live.

HYMN - 3
1.   Make me a captive, Lord, And then I shall be free;
     Force me to render up my sword, And I shall conquer or be
     I sink in life’s alarms
     When by myself I stand
     Imprison me within Thine arms
     And strong shall be my hand.
2.   My heart is weak and poor Until its master find;
     It has no spring of action sure
     It varies with the wind
     It cannot freely move,
     Till Thou hast wrought its chain;
     Enslave it with Thy matchless love,
     And deathless it shall reign.
3.   My power is faint and low
     Till have learned to serve
     It wants the needed fire to glow;
     It wants the breeze to nerve;
     It cannot drive the world
     Until itself be driven
     Its flag can only be unfurled
     When Thou shall breathe from heaven.
4.   My will is not my own
     Till Thou hast made it Thine;
     If it would reach a monarch’s throne
     It must its crown resign
     It only stands unbent
     Amid the clashing strife,
     When on Thy bosom it has learnt
     And found in Thee its life.
