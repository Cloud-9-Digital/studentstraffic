I. General
1.   National Institutes of Technology (NITs), Indian Institute of Engineering
     Science and Technology (IIEST), Indian Institutes of Information Technology
     (IIITs), Schools of Planning and Architecture (SPAs), a few other technical
     Institutes funded fully or partially by the central or a state government (Other-
     GFTIs) and a few other Technical Institutes offer academic programs for
     Foreign Nationals / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) / Persons of Indian Origin
     (PIOs) / Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) under Direct Admission of Students
     Abroad (DASA) Scheme of Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of
     India for which the admission is on the basis of JEE (Main) 2026.

     The allocation of seats to the participating institutes is carried out through a
     common process conducted by CSAB 2026 in accordance with Information
     Brochure DASA 2026 (UG).

                                     Eligibility Table
                                Qualifying Examination
       JEE (Main) 2026 Paper 1 (B.E. / B. Tech.)
       JEE (Main) 2026 Paper 2A (B. Arch.)
       JEE (Main) 2026 Paper 2B (B. Planning)
       Qualifying in the examinations mentioned above does not guarantee
       admission in any of the institutes. Each institute / academic program may
       have additional eligibility requirements. Candidates have to meet all the
       eligibility requirements to obtain admission.

     The names of NITs, IIEST, IIITs, Other-GFTIs, and a few other Technical
     Institutes are listed in Annexure 1.

     It is mandatory for the candidates to register through “DASA and CSAB
     Special” Seat Allocation portal to obtain seats to be allocated through
     DASA 2026 in any of the NITs, IIEST, IIITs, Other-GFTIs, and a few
     other Technical Institutes as mentioned in Annexure 1. The portal can be
                                         1
     reached at https://csab.nic.in.
     The academic programs, along with the category-wise intake capacity known
     as seat matrix, will be announced on the online portal.

     The information brochure stated herein will be strictly followed for allocating
     seats for the above-referenced academic programs for the academic year 2026-
     27, through DASA seat allocation scheme for the NITs, IIEST, IIITs, Other-
     GFTIs, and a few other Technical Institutes.

     Seats to be allocated for admission through DASA scheme will be
     supernumerary. The total number of supernumerary seats allocated in each of
     the above institutes for admission to a program through DASA scheme is
     limited to up to 15% of the total number of seats of the respective institutes,
     program-wise.

     There are various provisions contained in this Information Brochure
     which may not be reversible. The candidates are strongly advised to
     exercise their options carefully within the stipulated timeframe and may
     seek guidance through the grievance redressal mechanism (see Section
     XII) provided in this Information Brochure.

     The seat allocation process follows a strict timeline. The entire process is
     on-line and computer operated. No exceptions are possible and therefore,
     candidates are strongly advised to thoroughly acquaint themselves with
     the rules described in this Information Brochure, be vigilant, keep their
     documents handy and make arrangements for to pay the requisite fee and
     complete the requisite formalities, well in advance. It will not be possible
     to entertain requests for extension of time for completing any activity.

2.   A candidate chooses and applies to a series of academic programs in a certain
     order of preference.

3.   In the context of seat allocation, “academic program” refers to the combination
                                        2
      of academic program and institute. For example, a 4-year B.Tech. degree in
      Computer Science and Technology at IIEST, Shibpur is referred to as an
      “academic program”.


      A seat to the highest possible choice of academic program is allocated to the
      candidate considering the following factors:

      (i)   The candidate’s category tag [DASA (Non-SAARC), DASA (SAARC),
            DASA-CIWG] (see Section IV).

      (ii) The position of the candidate in the Common Rank List (CRL).
      (iii) The order of preference of academic programs selected by the candidate.
      (iv) The availability of seats in different seat categories / seat quotas for which
            the candidate is eligible.
      (v) Other rules as mentioned in this document.

III. Who can participate in the DASA seat allocation process
  4.    Eligibility Criteria

  Nationality: Candidates who are of Foreign Nationals or Overseas Citizens of India
  (OCI) or Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) or Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are
  eligible. Please refer to Annexure 8 for the definition of OCI/PIO (I) and OCI /PIO
  (F) nationality status.
  Age: Candidates born on or after October 01, 2001, are eligible. Date of birth as
  recorded in the Secondary Education Board / University Certificate [Class X or
  equivalent] or any certificate issued by the Government authorities only will be
  taken as authentic.
  Academic Eligibility: Candidate should have valid JEE (Main) 2026 score card
  and rank(s) of Paper 1 (B.E./B. Tech.) for B.E./B.Tech. programs and/or Paper 2A
  (B. Arch.) for B.Arch. programs and/or Paper 2B (B. Plan.) for B. Plan. program.
  In addition, candidates must satisfy the minimum percentage requirement in Class
  XII (that is Senior Secondary [10+2]) or equivalent [See Annexure 2] from any
  system of education as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities
  (www.aiu.ac.in) with specified subjects as mentioned below. Candidates appearing
  for Class XII examination or equivalent in the academic year 2025-26 and
  expecting their final results latest by September 30, 2026, may also apply.
       • For B.E./B.Tech.: Candidates should have successfully completed
          Mathematics, Physics and one of the subjects from (Chemistry,
          Biotechnology, Computer Science, Biology) in Class XII with 75% marks
          (CGPA of 7.5 out of 10) in aggregate in 5 subjects, as applicable in the
          respective boards. Please refer to Annexure 2(b) of JoSAA 2026 Business
          Rule (https://josaa.nic.in).
       • For B.Arch.: No candidate shall be admitted to architecture course unless
                                            4
         she/he has passed 10+2 scheme of examination with Physics, Chemistry and
         Mathematics or passed 10+3 diploma examination with mathematics as
         compulsory subject. The candidate should have secured 75% marks (7.5
         CGPA out 10) in aggregate in the qualifying examination in 5 subjects, as
         applicable in the respective boards. Please refer to Annexure 2(b) of JoSAA
         2026 Business Rule (https://josaa.nic.in).
      • For B. Plan.: No candidate shall be admitted to planning course unless she/he
         has passed a 10+2 scheme of examination with mathematics. The candidate
         should have secured 75% marks (OR 7.5 CGPA out of 10) in aggregate in
         the qualifying examination in 5 subjects, as applicable in the respective
         boards. Please refer to Annexure 2(b) of JoSAA 2026 Business Rule
         (https://josaa.nic.in).
 Residential Requirement: All foreign nationals studying in any country including
 India or Indian nationals studying abroad who fulfill prescribed admission criteria
 shall be eligible. Prescribed admission criteria for Indian Nationals or OCI/PIO (I)
 shall be at least two years of education, inclusive of 11th and 12th or equivalent (not
 beyond), in a foreign country during the last 8 years and must pass the same from
 abroad.
 Candidates eligible for both DASA 2026 (UG) and CSAB Special Rounds 2026 are
 advised to refer the document entitled Important Information for candidates

IV. Categorization of seats and candidates
 5.    All available seats in the DASA scheme are referred to as DASA-GEN seats.
       However, 5% seats (within 15% of the supernumerary quota of DASA) are
       reserved for Children of Indian Workers in Gulf (CIWG) scheme. In other
       words, one- third of DASA seats are reserved for CIWG. Such seats are
       referred to as CIWG seats. All DASA-GEN seats other than CIWG seats are
       referred to as Non-CIWG seats. During seat allocation at Round-II, any vacant

                                           5
     seat out of the 5% reserved for CIWG shall be converted to Non-CIWG seats.

     The following Gulf countries fall under CIWG: United Arab Emirates,
     Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia.

6.   In order to avail the benefit of CIWG, either of the parents of the candidate
     must be working or have worked in a Gulf country till any date of the year
     2026. Documents to be produced by the candidate are enumerated in
     Annexure 5.

7.   For the purpose of seat allocation, each qualified candidate is categorized
     based on nationality as given below:

     (i)   Category tag DASA (SAARC): This is assigned to Foreign Nationals /
           Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) / Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs), who
           are nationals of SAARC countries (except India).
     (ii) Category tag DASA (Non - SAARC): This is assigned to Foreign
           Nationals / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) / Persons of Indian Origin
           (PIOs), who are NOT nationals of SAARC countries. This tag is also
           assigned to Non-Resident Indians (NRI).
     (iii) Category tag DASA-CIWG: This is assigned to Non- Resident Indians
           (NRIs) whose either OR both of the parents are working in gulf country
           [United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi
           Arabia].
     NRI candidates who are eligible for CIWG seats will have the option to be
     considered for Non-CIWG seats as well under DASA (Non-SAARC) category
     tag only. Further details in this regard are delineated in Clauses 12 and 27.

     Candidates eligible for both DASA 2026 (UG) and CSAB Special Rounds
     2026 are advised to refer to the document titled Important Information for
     candidates registering for both DASA 2026 (UG) and CSAB Special Rounds
     2026, which is available in the website https://csab.nic.in.

(i) Registration

10. Once logged in, all eligible candidates must register for DASA 2026 seat
    allocation as per the schedule (Annexure 3). In the online portal, each
    candidate will be asked a set of questionnaires, viz. nationality, OCI/PIO
    status, CIWG status, etc. At the time of registration, candidates will NOT be
    allowed to change their personal details such as name, father’s / mother’s /
    guardian’s name, date of birth, and Class XII (or equivalent) examination
    board. However, candidates can change their nationality, year and place as
    well as status of passing class XII (subject to conditions specified in Clause
    59), and gender data. Note that the candidates whose documents are already
    verified at NIT+ system during JoSAA 2026 rounds cannot update their
    personal details including nationality and Class XII (or equivalent) passing
    status. In addition, candidates whose documents are verified at the IIT system
    during JoSAA 2026, cannot update their nationality.

    NRI or OCI/PIO candidates who have participated in JoSAA-2026 and have
    been allotted seats in NIT+ system and have already SECURED ADMISSION
    at the allotted institute by paying the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF) and Partial
    Admission Fee (PAF), are also eligible for DASA seat allocation provided
    they fulfill the criteria as given in Section III. These candidates will be
    participating in DASA Rounds as fresh candidates after payment of required
    DASA Enrollment Fee (DEF) as mentioned in Clause 11 while retaining their
    seats for which they have paid PAF during JoSAA 2026 rounds. In case, a new
    seat is allotted in DASA Rounds, then their retained seats will
    AUTOMATICALLY GET CANCELLED, and candidates will not have
    any claim on the previously secured/retained seats. The declaration regarding
    the same is to be submitted at the time of registration for DASA 2026. The
    format of the declaration is provided in Annexure 4 of this document. For
                                       8
      these candidates, balance amount (if any) will be refunded after adjustment as
      mentioned in Annexure 6.

      Candidates, who have secured admission against allocated IIT seats during
      JoSAA rounds, are also eligible to register for DASA 2026 seat allocation
      process as fresh candidates after payment of DEF as mentioned in Clause 11,
      provided they meet the eligibility requirement of DASA. Such candidates may
      note that securing an IIT seat during the JoSAA rounds will prevent them from
      appearing for JEE (Advanced) 2027. Please refer to Clause 11 of Information
      Brochure of JEE (Advance) 2026 and Rule 78 of JoSAA 2026 Business Rules.

(iv) Document Verification and Respond to queries (if any)
    21. At the time of document verification, document verifying officer may raise
         query(ies), which will be shown on the candidate’s portal; the candidate must
         respond to the same in online mode (through the candidate's portal) within the
         stipulated time. Failure to respond to the query(ies) on time will lead to
         cancellation of the candidature and the candidate will no longer be able to
         participate in the seat allocation process of DASA 2026. It is the
         responsibility of the candidate to check the online portal at regular intervals of
         time and respond to queries (if any) without fail within the stipulated time. No
         separate intimation will be given to the candidates in this regard.

    22. During the process of document verification, if there is any mismatch between
         the document uploaded earlier by the candidate during JEE (Main) 2026
         registration and the document uploaded by the candidate on the seat allocation
         portal, and the same is found to be due to willful forgery/act of cheating, the
         offer of admission will be withdrawn and the candidate shall be debarred from
         further rounds of seat allocation. Thus, the candidate will forfeit the eligibility
         for admissions to any of the institutes through DASA 2026. The decision of
         Chairman, DASA 2026 on this issue shall be final and binding.

    23. If a foreign national fail to produce valid proof of foreign nationality (i.e.,
         foreign passport or citizenship certificate), the candidature of the candidate
         will be cancelled.

    24. In case of any discrepancies in the uploaded documents noticed during the
         online document verification process, query (ies) may be raised and will be
         notified to the candidate only on the online portal. Candidates must respond to
         the query(ies) (by uploading new/modified document(s), if required) within
         the last date/time specified for the given round [see Annexure 3]. The
         candidates are advised to check the online portal regularly for queries, if any.
         Additional query(ies) may be raised in case discrepancy(ies) still persist(s). In
         such cases, the candidate must respond to the additional query(ies) within the
                                             12
     last date/time specified for the given round [Annexure 3]. If all the queries
     are resolved based on the validity of the document(s) uploaded and the
     candidate meets all the eligibility criteria then she/he will be allowed for
     choice filling and seat allocation process.

     Failure to reply to the query(ies) raised would lead to cancellation of the
     candidature. In such cases, the candidate will NOT be considered for the
     DASA 2026 seat allocation process.

25. Verification Center shall verify the uploaded documents and ensure that the
     candidate meets all the eligibility criteria. If all the documents are found to be
     valid and all the eligibility criteria are met by the candidate, the candidate will
     be allowed for choice filling and seat allocation process.

26. Seat will be cancelled if, at any time, any of the documents / certificates is/are
     found to be invalid / fraudulent and/or the candidate does not meet all / any of
     the eligibility norms.

27. During the document verification of DASA-CIWG category candidates who
     have not opted to be considered for Non-CIWG seats (under DASA (Non-
     SAARC) category), if their CIWG status could not be established based on the
     submitted documents, then the candidate will be asked on the candidate portal
     if she/he wants her/his candidature to be considered for Non-CIWG seats
     (under DASA (Non-SAARC) category). Upon confirmation, the candidate
     must pay the Balance DEF (Difference amount of DEF for DASA (non-
     SAARC) and DASA-CIWG) and fill in the choices of Non-CIWG seats,
     which will be considered for seat allocation. If the candidate declines to
     consider herself/himself for Non-CIWG seats or does not confirm or fails to
     pay the Balance DEF in the stipulated time as mentioned in Annexure 3,
     her/his candidature will not be considered for any of the DASA seats. Refund,
     if any, shall be governed by the rules laid in Annexure 6.

(vi) Online Choice Filling and Locking

30. After successful fee payment and document verification, candidates are
    required to fill in their CHOICES of academic programs. Candidates are
    advised to carefully fill their CHOICES and save them frequently during the
    process. In case, choices filled in are not saved by the candidate, these will
    NOT be auto-saved by the system at the end of the choice filling stage and the
    unsaved choices shall not be considered for seat allocation. If the candidate
    has not saved at least one choice then she/he will NOT be considered for any
    seat allocation. The registered candidates whose documents are verified
    successfully, will be able to fill in their choices. Candidates are also advised
    to check the special eligibility criteria, if any, for the academic programs
    before making their choices. A list of such criteria is available on the website.

VI. Willingness options during the rounds
 40. When a candidate is allocated a seat for the first time, during online reporting
      she/he has to opt for any one of the willingness options, i.e., ‘Freeze’, ‘Float’,
      ‘Slide’, ‘Surrender’, ‘Withdraw’, which will be applicable for the next round
      (if any) of seat allocation. The definition of each of these willingness terms is
      given in this clause. Candidates who are not exercising the available
      willingness option, will be AUTOMATICALLY MOVED to the next round
      (if any) with ‘Float’ as their DEFAULT willingness option. However, if s/he
      is allocated the seat of first choice then the default willingness option will be
      ‘Freeze’.

      (A) Freeze:
      Choosing the option “Freeze” means that the candidate accepts the offered

                                          17
seat and indicates that the candidate is satisfied with the allocated academic
program and that the candidate does not want to participate in further
round of seat allocation while retaining the same seat. Such candidates will
NOT be considered in subsequent round of seat allocation, if any. Their
chosen seat shall remain allocated to them and shall be confirmed.

(B) Float:
Choosing the option “Float” means that the candidate accepts the offered seat
and indicates that, if an academic program of higher preference in any
Institute is offered in subsequent round of seat allocation, the candidate will
accept it. Else, the candidate will continue with the currently accepted
academic program. Such candidates will be considered in subsequent rounds
of seat allocation, if any.

(C) Slide:
Choosing the option “Slide” means that the candidate accepts the offered seat
and indicates that, if an academic program of higher preference is offered
within the same Institute in subsequent round of seat allocation, the candidate
will accept it. Such candidates will be considered in subsequent rounds of seat
allocation, if any.

(D) Surrender:
Candidate REJECTS the allotted seat and wishes to participate in next Round
of seat allotment. Hence the allotted seat will be cancelled. Allotment in the
next round will be considered from the choices higher than the surrendered
seat. The candidates who have secured a seat by paying PAF at the end of
JoSAA 2026 rounds and have participated in “DASA and CSAB-Special”
seat allocation process 2026 can also exercise ‘surrender’ option. In such
cases, the candidate will lose the seat irrespective of whether she/he had
secured the seat in JoSAA rounds or “DASA and CSAB Special Rounds
2026”. ‘Surrender’ option will not be available in the last round of seat
allocation.
                                  18
     (E) Withdraw:
     Candidate REJECTS the allotted/accepted seat and does not wish to
     participate   in   subsequent   rounds    of    seat   allotment.   Hence   the
     allotted/accepted seat will be cancelled irrespective whether the seat was
     accepted during JoSAA rounds or it is allotted/accepted in “DASA and CSAB
     Special Rounds 2026”. The candidate will be out of seat allocation process,
     i.e., she/he shall not be considered for subsequent round of seat allocation, if
     any. Further details on the ‘Withdraw’ option are elaborated in Section VIII.

41. In case the candidate has been allocated a seat as per the candidate’s first
     choice, the candidate will not be shown ‘Float’, ‘Slide’, and ‘Surrender’
     willingness options. In other words, the candidate has been allocated the best
     choice as per her/his choice list and therefore, she/he must exercise the
     willingness option of ‘Freeze’ in order to retain the seat. Candidates who are
     not exercising the willingness option in this case, the default willingness
     option will be ‘Freeze’ for these candidates.

42. In case the candidate has been allocated a seat which is other than the
     candidate’s first choice and the candidate wants to retain the seat then she/he
     MUST choose the ‘Freeze’ option. In case the candidate wants to be
     considered for a higher choice in her/his list of choices by RETAINING the
     allocated seat, in subsequent rounds, the candidate MUST choose one of the
     available options (i.e., ‘Float’, ‘Slide’). If the candidate wishes to be
     considered for a higher choice in her/his list of choices by REJECTING the
     currently allocated seat, the candidate can choose the ‘Surrender’ option.
     Candidates who are not exercising the available willingness option, will be
     AUTOMATICALLY MOVED to the next round (if any) with ‘Float’ as their
     DEFAULT willingness option. However, if s/he is allocated the seat of first
     choice then the default willingness option will be ‘Freeze’.

43. For candidates who choose the ‘Float’ or ‘Slide’ option, allocation of a seat in
     an academic program of the candidate’s higher preference would

                                        19
       automatically result in the replacement of the seat accepted by the candidate
       in the earlier round. Candidates will not have any claims on the previously
       allocated seats.
       (Candidates are thus advised to fill in their list of choices carefully and

  44. In the last round, all allotments are presumed to be accepted with the ‘Freeze’

  45. If a candidate opts ‘Freeze’ against a seat, the option cannot be changed to

       options. Similarly, candidates exercising the ‘Slide’ option cannot change to

                                                                    Annexure 8
                  Foreign Nationals and OCI/PIO Candidates

A.    Foreign Nationals
Candidates who are NOT citizens of India (by birth or naturalized) shall be
considered as foreign nationals. Such candidates are also eligible for seat
allocation through DASA 2026 Seat Allocation Process.

B.    OCI/PIO Candidates
In light of the recent judicial pronouncement1 concerning OCI/PIO candidates, the
following provisions are made for such candidates.
OCI/PIO candidates who have obtained their OCI/PIO cards before 04.03.2021
have the option of registration for JoSAA 2026 EITHER as (a) foreign nationals
OR (b) at par with Indian nationals. Accordingly, OCI/PIO candidates are divided
into three categories as follows:
(i)   Those OCI/PIO candidates who have obtained their OCI/PIO cards
      subsequent to 04.03.20212 will be considered as foreign national candidates
      and will be governed by the eligibility conditions contained herein for the
      foreign national candidates. For ease of convenience, these candidates are
      referred to as OCI/PIO (F). The OCI/PIO (F) candidates are also eligible for
      seat allocation.
(ii) Those OCI/PIO candidates who have obtained their OCI/PIO cards before
      04.03.2021 AND choose to be considered as foreign nationals, will be
      governed by the eligibility conditions contained herein for the foreign
      national candidates and are also referred to as OCI/PIO (F). The OCI/PIO (F)
      candidates will also be eligible for seat allocation.
(iii) Those OCI/PIO candidates who have obtained their OCI/PIO cards before
      04.03.2021 AND choose to be treated at par with Indian nationals, will be



                                          46
governed by the eligibility conditions contained herein for the Indian national
candidates and are referred to as OCI/PIO (I).
OCI/PIO candidates who neither registered for JEE (Advanced) 2026 nor for
JoSAA 2026 and have obtained their OCI/PIO cards before 04.03.2021 are
advised to choose their option (OCI/PIO (I) or OCI/PIO (F)) carefully. These
candidates have to exercise their options while registering for “DASA and CSAB-
Special” Seat Allocation 2026.

OCI/PIO candidates who have registered for JEE (Advanced) 2026 and/or
document verification not done during JoSAA 2026 rounds and have obtained
their OCI/PIO cards before 04.03.2021 will be asked to confirm their choice of
OCI/PIO(I) or OCI/PIO(F) while registering for “DASA and CSAB-Special” Seat
Allocation 2026.

OCI/PIO candidates whose documents have been verified during JoSAA 2026
rounds are NOT allowed to change their nationality for the purposes of the
DASA/CSAB Special Rounds 2026.

The options once exercised by the candidate cannot be changed and shall remain
the same for all further seat allocation processes.
