9. Hostel Leave and Entry Guidelines:
Leave Permissions:
             o Hostel students shall not leave the campus without prior written permission from the
                 concerned Warden if leaving after 7:00 PM.
             o Students intending to remain absent from the hostel during the night (between 9:00 PM and
                 6:00 AM) must obtain prior written permission from the Warden.
     2. Emergency Leave:
             o In case of emergencies, students should immediately inform the concerned Warden and obtain
                 written permission.
     3. Application Process:
             o Students must apply for leave in advance, clearly stating:
                      Reason for leaving
                      Address of the destination
             o A leave register or an online system should be maintained to record permissions.
     4. Entry Recording:
             o Students must sign an entry/exit register when leaving or returning to the hostel, especially for
                 after-hours movement.
