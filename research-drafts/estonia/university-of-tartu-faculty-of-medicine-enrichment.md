# University of Tartu Medicine enrichment

**Status:** held for final packaging; core 2026/27 evidence captured.

## Decision summary

University of Tartu’s Medicine curriculum is a six-year, 360-ECTS integrated bachelor’s and master’s programme in Tartu. It is taught in English and leads to the Degree in Medicine (MD). The 2026/27 intake has 24 places and tuition of EUR 13,200 per year. Estonian language courses are compulsory because clinical communication with local patients requires Estonian.

## Admissions

For international applicants and Estonian applicants who graduated abroad, the 2026 process uses DreamApply: applications open 2 January and close 15 April. Required submissions include the online application, English motivation letter, officially certified secondary-school certificate and grade list, certified English translations, passport copy, English-language proof and the EUR 100 application-fee receipt unless the previous study level was completed in Estonia.

Selection is based on the motivation letter (20%), online physics and chemistry test (60%) and interview (20%). The test and motivation letter must each score at least 51/100 before an applicant can be considered for interview. The 2026 test is scheduled for 15 May and interviews for 20–22 May. UT states results on 1 June and the academic year begins on 31 August.

## Teaching and clinical training

The University describes the programme as fully English taught. Its Faculty of Medicine includes clinical, family-medicine/public-health and biomedical institutes. Clinical learning uses Tartu University Hospital, Estonia’s largest healthcare provider and the country’s only academic hospital. The University says the hospital supervises medical students and residents. The curriculum includes compulsory Estonian courses for communication with local patients.

## Fees and funding

Annual 2026/27 tuition is EUR 13,200. The fee is paid by semester. EU/EEA/Swiss citizens pay the first semester after arrival; admitted students from other countries prepay half of the first-semester tuition after accepting the offer and after UT receives hard-copy documents. UT issues the official admission letter required for visa use after the prepayment is received.

The University’s policy says new applicants from outside the EU/EEA will not receive tuition-waiver places from 2026/27. The collected sources do not establish a Medicine-specific scholarship or loan package. Housing and living costs are separate from tuition.

## Accommodation and support

UT has student dormitories for international and local students, managed by Tartu Student Village. New international degree students are invited to an orientation course. This packet does not state a guaranteed room, room price or a Medicine-specific housing allocation.

## Recognition and professional pathway

UT says graduates receive an MD, can work in a position requiring a doctor’s qualification and can continue to residency in Estonia or abroad or doctoral study. UT received seven-year institutional accreditation in 2022 from the Estonian Quality Agency; this establishes institutional quality assurance, not a guarantee of a licence or residency in another country.

## FAQs

### Is the programme taught in English?

Yes. The Medicine curriculum is fully in English, and Estonian is compulsory for local patient communication.

### How long is the MD?

Six years and 360 ECTS.

### What is the 2026/27 tuition?

EUR 13,200 per year, paid by semester.

### What are the admissions components?

Motivation letter, online physics and chemistry test, and entrance interview, weighted 20%, 60% and 20% respectively.

### Can I transfer into a later year?

UT says its course system and curriculum differences make a full transfer unlikely; applicants are directed to apply for first year, with possible credit transfer after admission.

### Does the MD guarantee licensing abroad?

No. Licensing and residency rules are set by the destination regulator and are not guaranteed by admission or graduation.
