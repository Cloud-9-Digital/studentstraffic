# Université des Antilles — Martinique medical pathway enrichment

**Status:** held for targeted evidence completion, not publication.

## Decision summary

Université des Antilles operates its UFR Santé in Martinique and Guadeloupe. The Faculty states that its complete medicine curriculum has operated since September 2023. The documented pathway begins with PASS or LAS access, continues through DFGSM and the second cycle, and includes the medical third cycle (internat). This is a French public-university medical route, not a standalone English-language Caribbean MD product.

**Best next action:** Get my Martinique medicine pathway assessment.

## Programme and clinical learning

The UFR Santé describes PASS as the entry route to medicine and identifies DFGSM as years two and three of the first medical cycle. It also lists the medical internat and says the second medical cycle opened in September 2023, completing the curriculum. The University’s health-learning library includes Martinique CHU emergency-ward content and training material on DFGSM/DFASM placements, which supports a hospital-linked learning environment without claiming a particular placement for every applicant.

## Admissions and eligibility

For the Martinique PASS route, the University’s public Parcoursup material says that entry to medicine is through PASS or LAS. PASS students must pass the disciplinary minor; this permits them to present for MMOP admission interviews. Students who are not admitted can continue into year two of the partner licence. The University sets the number of places after the required regional health-agency process.

This establishes a selective progression route rather than direct automatic admission to medicine. A complete applicant assessment needs the candidate’s school qualification, current country of study, academic record and intended entry year.

The University’s 2026–2027 registration page says registration fees are set nationally and vary by award and student nationality. ## Candidate FAQs

### Is medicine offered in Martinique by Université des Antilles?

Yes. The UFR Santé states that it operates in Martinique and that the complete medical curriculum has been offered since September 2023.

### How do students enter medicine?

The documented entry routes are PASS and LAS. PASS progression requires the disciplinary minor to be passed before the student can present for MMOP admission interviews.

### Does PASS guarantee entry to medicine?

No. PASS is an access route; the University describes selection for second-year MMOP places and a continuation option in the partner licence for students who are not admitted.

### What is the tuition fee?

The University applies nationally set registration fees that vary by award and nationality. A complete medical-route and applicant-specific cost plan is not available in the sources collected for this draft.

### Is the course taught in English?

The collected materials do not establish a formal teaching-language policy or English track. This draft does not present the programme as English taught.

### Does the medical route include clinical training?

The Faculty lists the second cycle and medical internat, while the University’s health-learning library includes Martinique CHU and medical-placement training material. Individual placement arrangements are not stated here.
