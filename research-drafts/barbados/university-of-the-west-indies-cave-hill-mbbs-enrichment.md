# UWI Cave Hill MBBS enrichment

**Status:** held for the next verified MBBS admissions cycle; not publication-ready.

## Decision summary

The University of the West Indies, Cave Hill offers a five-year MBBS organised in two phases: three years of Phase I and two years of Phase II. Phase I uses an integrated, system-based approach across basic medical sciences and patient-centred disciplines. Phase II consists of rotations through the main clinical disciplines. The programme page identifies the award as MBBS and the degree as undergraduate.

## Learning and clinical training

During the first three years, the official programme description says modules integrate basic medical science with clinical, patient-centred disciplines. In the final two years, students rotate through the main clinical disciplines with general training emphasis. The Faculty lists postgraduate progression options including Anaesthesia, Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, General Surgery, Orthopaedic Surgery, Ophthalmology, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Psychiatry and Family Medicine. These are progression options, not a promised residency placement.

## Fees and funding

The 2025/26 undergraduate fee booklet is the current official MBBS financial source. It distinguishes MBBS pre-clinical and clinical fee treatment, but this packet does not calculate an all-inclusive five-year cost and must not publish a single total. The fee state remains **on request** until the current cycle’s MBBS schedule, mandatory charges, housing, insurance, travel and clinical-phase costs are assembled.

For continuing students, the Cave Hill Financial Aid Programme offers partial tuition-fee waivers to eligible CARICOM nationals in good academic standing with demonstrated financial need; first-year students are not eligible. The award cannot be applied to economic costs or registration fees and is capped at BBD 3,000 per semester. The 2026/27 scholarship page lists Medical Sciences eligibility for specific continuing-student awards, including the UWI Toronto Gala Scholarship and AFUWI Scholarship, with their stated nationality, year and GPA criteria.

## FAQs

### How long is the MBBS at UWI Cave Hill?

The programme lasts five years: six Phase I semesters followed by four Phase II semesters.

### Are MBBS applications open for 2026/27?

No. Cave Hill’s admissions page lists MBBS among programmes not open for the 2026/27 cycle.

### What is the learning structure?

Phase I integrates basic medical science with patient-centred disciplines. Phase II uses rotations through the main clinical disciplines.

### What does the MBBS cost?

An official 2025/26 fee booklet is available, but the current packet does not establish a complete five-year cost. A route-specific cost and funding plan is required.

### Can an MBBS student apply for financial aid?

The financial-aid programme is for eligible continuing CARICOM undergraduate students; it excludes first-year students and has nationality, income and academic-standing conditions.

### Does the MBBS guarantee a medical licence or residency?

No. This draft does not make licensing or residency guarantees.
